# Jobs Gallery

> The new Job Gallery feature on the Job Details page brings all your job-related media into one seamless, unified view.

The **Gallery** feature in Zuper provides a centralized, visual workspace for managing all job-related media. Instead of navigating through multiple sections such as Checklists, Notes, or Attachments, users can now view every photo and video associated with a job in one organized location.

**Prerequisite**: Ensure that the “**Enable Job Gallery**” setting is toggled On in the Job Settings under the General tab. You can access this by navigating to **Settings** from the left navigation menu, then selecting **Modules** > **Jobs** > **General Job Settings**.

## Master Tags

Before exploring the Gallery feature in the Job Details page, it’s important to set up Master Tags to keep the Job Gallery structured, searchable, and easy to navigate.

Without standardized tags, the Gallery can become cluttered and hard to manage. Master Tags ensure consistent labeling, cleaner organization, and easier filtering- making it simple for teams to find the images they need.

### Creating Master Tags

1. Navigate to the **Settings** module from the left navigation menu.
2. Click **Miscellaneous** and select **Master Tags**. The Master Tags listing page appears, displaying existing tags along with the modules they are associated with.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/V1EM1k8xihAz_KKS/images/mastertags.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=V1EM1k8xihAz_KKS&q=85&s=97416e055771800c1daa67ad5f356c88" alt="Mastertags Pn" width="1920" height="660" data-path="images/mastertags.png" />

3. Click **+ New Tag** to create a new tag.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/_yG2Rlhrbn1yzW8I/images/Galw0.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=_yG2Rlhrbn1yzW8I&q=85&s=dafaa003cb2a9c1834217e3df87a1a22" alt="Galw0 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Galw0.png" />

4. Enter the following details:
   * **Tag Name:** Provide a unique tag name. *(Mandatory)*
   * **Choose Module:** Select **Gallery** from the dropdown. *(Mandatory)*
   * **Description:** (Optional) Add a brief description for reference.
5. Click **Create** to add the tag.

## Available Actions within the Gallery

The **Gallery** feature allows you to view, organize, and manage all media associated with a job. You can filter, download, group, and update media details for better organization and accessibility.

### Filtering Media

You can refine the media displayed using filters:

* **Select Date Range** – filter media by a specific date range.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/I54PQ5mXfkbrYo_j/images/gallerydate.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=I54PQ5mXfkbrYo_j&q=85&s=1e3e1b39914b80cc5b472670ffec4b75" alt="Gallerydate Pn" width="1447" height="840" data-path="images/gallerydate.png" />

* **Filter icon** – refine media further by:
  1. **Type** – Photo, Video, or Any Type.
  2. **User** – media uploaded by specific users.
  3. **Tags** – filter media by assigned tags.
  4. Show Related:

The Show related toggle expands the gallery to include media from other jobs linked to the same contact or property as the current job. When you turn on Show related, photos and videos from those related jobs appear alongside the current job's media, grouped by date. This makes it easy to compare documentation across multiple visits or service calls for the same customer, without leaving the gallery.<br /> • Tap Show related to turn it on. The chip highlights blue when active, and the gallery reloads to include related media.<br /> • Tap Show related again to turn it off and return to the current job's media only.

**To use Show Related**

1. Select the **Jobs** module from the left navigation menu and open a job.
2. Under Navigation, select the **Gallery** tab.
3. Select the **Filter icon** in the toolbar above the media grid.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/DjOgAEYl8c23z5ve/images/Showr1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=DjOgAEYl8c23z5ve&q=85&s=893830d47eeae385dcdf1b6eea0cb899" alt="Showr1" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Showr1.png" />

4. A filter panel opens. Locate the Show Related row at the bottom of the panel.
5. Toggle Show Related to on. The switch turns blue.
6. The gallery refreshes. Media from the associated customer record appears alongside the current job's media, grouped by date.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/DjOgAEYl8c23z5ve/images/Showr2-1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=DjOgAEYl8c23z5ve&q=85&s=4d29edf03df507a1e8673975f6775dd2" alt="Showr2 1" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Showr2-1.png" />

Use the Type, User, or Tags filters together with Show Related to narrow the results further.

### Move or associate photos to another job

Keep job photos connected to the right work without downloading and re-uploading them. You can move photos into a different job's gallery, or associate them so they appear in both galleries at once.

* **Move to Job** – Removes the photos from this job's gallery, then places them in the gallery of the job you choose.
* **Associate to Job** – Adds the photos to the gallery of the job you choose, while keeping them in this job's gallery as well.

<Note>
  Tags, descriptions, comments, and comment replies carry over when you associate a photo with another job. If you edit any of these afterward, the change appears in every gallery the photo belongs to.
</Note>

1. Open the job and select the **Gallery** tab.
2. Select the photos you want to move or associate. A bottom action bar appears, displaying the number of selected items.
3. Select **More** from the bottom action bar.
4. Select **Move to Job** or **Associate to Job**.
5. In the job selection panel, select the job you want.

<Tip>
  By default, the panel lists jobs for the same customer as the current job. To choose a job for a different customer, select the **X** on the **Filter By Contact** field. Then search for the job you want.
</Tip>

6. Select **Choose Job** to confirm, or select **Cancel** to close the panel without making changes.

## **Share the job gallery with customers**

You already know how to mark a photo Public or Internal. That same **Visibility** setting decides what a customer sees when you share the entire job gallery, not just one photo at a time.

Instead of sending photos one at a time, you can share the whole job gallery in a single link. Only photos and videos marked Public appear in that link, so you stay in control of what the customer sees, even after you share it.

**Gallery link and timeline link**

A **Gallery link** shares every Public photo and video in the job gallery, without showing dates. A **Timeline link** shares the same media sorted by upload date, giving the customer a chronological view of job progress. Each job has its own distinct Gallery link and Timeline link. Zuper does not share these links across jobs. Share the gallery

1. Open the job and select the **Gallery** tab.
2. Select the **Share** icon at the top of the gallery.
3. Select the **Share gallery** to share the complete gallery or select the link to share the chronological view.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/fAFG8qdUB_Yzk6Iy/images/shar1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=fAFG8qdUB_Yzk6Iy&q=85&s=a8aa417df430145c73c71719a7c99f78" alt="Shar1" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/shar1.png" />

4. If you selected **Share gallery**, choose a method: Email, Text Message, or Link.
5. Complete the required fields for the method you chose, then select Share.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/fAFG8qdUB_Yzk6Iy/images/shar2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=fAFG8qdUB_Yzk6Iy&q=85&s=5fcac60ad75e575b73ddee9c54f677d2" alt="Shar2" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/shar2.png" />

<Note>
  **Note**: Only photos and videos marked **Public** appear in the link you share. To make a photo visible to the customer, open it in the gallery and set its **Visibility** to **Public**. See “Viewing and updating media details” earlier in the article.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Note**: Admins control the default visibility for new uploads by setting the image to **public by default**. Find this setting in General job settings. Changing this setting does not affect photos already uploaded or links already shared.
</Note>

### **Disable the Gallery link**

If you no longer want a customer to view shared photos, select the "**Disable Gallery link"** from the **Share** icon menu. Every Gallery link already sent for that job stops working immediately. This applies whether it was sent by email, text message, or a copied link.

This filter is helpful when you want to focus on visual documentation (photos) or review recorded walkthroughs or evidence (videos).

## Removing photos from the gallery

The Jobs gallery lets you remove photos you no longer need without permanently deleting them. When you remove a photo from the gallery, Zuper moves it to a built-in album called **Deleted Items**. It stays in the deleted album until you choose to restore it.

<Note>
  **Note:** Removing a photo from the gallery does not affect checklists, inspection forms, or notes. Those resources remain unchanged.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Important:** The restore capability described in this article applies only to photos in the job gallery. Zuper permanently deletes photos removed from a customer, property, Photo Feed, or project gallery. You cannot restore them.
</Tip>

### Restore Photos from deleted items album

Every job has a predefined album called **Deleted Items**. This album holds all the photos removed from the job gallery. Photos stay in this album indefinitely and can be restored at any time.

### Open the Deleted Items album

1.     Open the job and go to **Gallery** > **Albums** > **Deleted Items**.

2.    Select the photo or photos you want to restore.

  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/le06GnMwaSDWsinF/images/rph3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=le06GnMwaSDWsinF&q=85&s=f66daaf2dda1496f7e9a9605ea1adef3" alt="Rph3" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/rph3.png" />

To view a photo at full size again, restore it to the gallery first. *The selected photos return to the job's main gallery view.*

**How photo removal affects checklists, notes, and forms**

When you add a gallery photo to a checklist, note, or inspection form using **Pick from Zuper Gallery**, the photo appears in both places. If you later remove that photo from the gallery, it moves to **Deleted Items**. The copy in the checklist, note, or form stays in place and is not affected.

The photo remains visible in the checklist, note, or form, but you cannot edit its tags, description, or annotations from there. To edit the photo again, restore it to the gallery first.

### Removing photos from Photo Feed

Photo Feed shows a collection of images across all jobs. If you remove a photo from **Photo Feed**, Zuper moves it to the **Deleted Items** album of the job it belongs to — not to a separate deleted folder. Restoring that photo returns it to that job's gallery.

<Note>
  **Note:** When a job photo is removed from anywhere in Zuper — including **Photo Feed**, the **customer gallery**, the **property gallery**, or the **project gallery** — it is removed from the job gallery and moved to that job's **Deleted Items** album, and the photos stay indefinite. You can restore it from there at any time.
</Note>

### Removing photos in customer, property, and project galleries

<Note>
  Removing a photo from a customer, property, or project gallery permanently deletes that photo. There is no Deleted Items album and no restore option in these galleries. This action cannot be undone.
</Note>

### Customer merge

When you merge two customer records, Zuper does not carry over media from the deleted customer's gallery. Only the media already in the retained customer's record remains after the merge.

## Filtering Media in the Job Gallery

The Job Gallery provides multiple filter options to help you quickly locate specific photos or videos associated with the job. You can apply one or more filters at the same time to refine the results.

**1. Filter by Media Type**

Use this filter to control the type of media displayed in the Gallery grid:

* **All Media** – Shows both photos and videos.
* **Photo** – Displays only image files captured or uploaded for the job.
* **Video** – Displays only video files added to the job.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Le-LRkTYJtdJrvfP/images/Allmedia.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Le-LRkTYJtdJrvfP&q=85&s=6fb0be1456924f42021a8c3092b8acbf" data-path="images/Allmedia.png" />

**2. Filter by Tags**

Tags help categorize images based on location, task, project, or custom labels added by the technician or admin.

* The filter panel displays all tags used within this specific job.
* You can select **one or multiple tags** to narrow down the media list.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/ygsIvXHjJIH-Neix/images/tagsfilter.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=ygsIvXHjJIH-Neix&q=85&s=f10e1cde5963a77c214a6b76d6366c4a" data-path="images/tagsfilter.png" />

* Only media items associated with the selected tags will be shown in the Gallery.

This is especially useful when searching for images such as **before/after**, **installation**, **inspection**, or **site-specific** photos.

**3. Filter by Date**

Use the Date filter to view media captured or uploaded within a specific timeframe. Available options include:

* **All**
* **Today**
* **Yesterday**
* **Last 7 Days**
* **Last 30 Days**
* **This Month**
* **Last Month**
* **Custom Date Range** – Select a start and end date based on your requirement.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/I54PQ5mXfkbrYo_j/images/filter.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=I54PQ5mXfkbrYo_j&q=85&s=69c6576d04cab3ac85b6783d27321a15" data-path="images/filter.png" />

4. Show related media

Turn on **Show related media** to also display photos and videos associated with this job from other jobs, alongside the media uploaded directly to this job. Turn it off to view only this job's own media.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/DjOgAEYl8c23z5ve/images/Show-related.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=DjOgAEYl8c23z5ve&q=85&s=d93a7cbc3503d33530e034e6b1cbcb50" alt="Show Related" width="1419" height="2796" data-path="images/Show-related.png" />

This filter helps technicians quickly retrieve images based on when the work was performed or documented.

**Clearing all filters**

When one or more filters are active, a **Clear all** button appears to the right of the filter chips. Tap **Clear all** to remove every active filter at once and return the gallery to its default unfiltered view.

**Filtered results update instantly**, ensuring you can efficiently locate the most relevant photos or videos during job execution, reporting, or customer interactions.

## Key Highlights

* Images captured from checklists, inspection forms, notes, or direct upload (online/offline) appear automatically in the Job Gallery.
* Tags are currently supported **only for images**.
* The Zuper Camera Component provides a unified tagging and preview experience across Gallery and Notes.
* Multiple tags can be applied to a single image for highly accurate categorization.
* Images uploaded to the Job Gallery are not automatically linked to the job's assets. To associate an image with an asset, navigate to the asset record and manually attach the image.
* The Image Tags feature in the Zuper Mobile Gallery transforms how field technicians and Admins capture, organize, and retrieve job-related images. This feature enhances documentation, streamlines workflows, and improves collaboration by enabling contextual tagging and role-based management.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I not see the Gallery tab?">
    The Gallery tab is disabled by default. To enable it, go to **Settings** > **Organization Settings** > **Job Settings** and turn on **Enable Job Gallery**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types does the Gallery support?">
    The **Gallery** displays standard media files such as JPG, PNG, and MP4. Other file types, for example PDFs, remain in their original sections and do not appear in the **Gallery**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I tag images after uploading them to the Gallery?">
    Yes. Admins can edit tags for any image in the **Gallery** by selecting the **Tag** icon on the image thumbnail and accessing **Tag Management**. Field Executives can reassign existing tags but cannot create new ones.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many tags can I apply to a single image?">
    There is no strict limit. Using three to five relevant tags per image keeps the **Gallery** organized and easy to search.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does removing a photo from the job gallery delete it permanently?">
    No. Removing a photo from the job gallery moves it to the **Deleted Items** album for that job, where it stays indefinitely. You can restore it to the main gallery at any time. Permanent deletion is not triggered by this action.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does removing a photo from the job gallery affect my checklists, notes, or inspection forms?">
    No. The photo remains visible in any checklist, note, or inspection form where it was added. However, you can no longer edit the photo's tags, description, or annotations from within those records once it has been removed from the gallery. Restore the photo to re-enable editing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where does a restored photo go?">
    A restored photo returns to the job's default gallery. It does not return to any custom album it was organized into before removal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a record of who removed a photo?">
    Yes. Every removal is recorded in the job's activity log. The log shows the action, the user who performed it, and the time it occurred.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I remove multiple photos at once?">
    Yes. Select the photos you want to remove using the checkboxes, then select **Delete** from the bottom action bar. You can also use **Select All** to remove all photos in view at once. The same bulk selection applies when restoring photos from the **Deleted Items** album.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I removed a photo from Photo Feed. Where did it go?">
    The photo moved to the **Deleted Items** album of the job it belongs to. Open that job, go to **Gallery** > **Albums** > **Deleted Items**, and restore it from there. It returns to the original job gallery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I removed a photo from the customer or project or property gallery. Can I restore it?">
    No. Photos removed from customer, property, or project galleries are permanently deleted. There is no **Deleted Items** album or restore option in these galleries.

    If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

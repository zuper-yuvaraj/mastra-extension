# Capturing Photos

> Powered by AI, this feature allows technicians to capture images and record voice input simultaneously during a job. Zuper AI then transcribes and summarizes the spoken content, combining it with the captured images to automatically generate a clear and structured job note.

The **AI Walkthrough Note** feature in the Zuper mobile app is designed to simplify and enhance job documentation for field technicians. Powered by advanced AI technology, this feature supports recording **videos, capturing photos, and recording audio** during a job. After the walkthrough session, Zuper AI automatically transcribes and summarizes the recorded audio, combining it with your captured media to create a clear, organized job note.

This eliminates manual data entry, reduces documentation time, and ensures accurate, professional records of the work performed.

In this guide, we will focus on **how to capture photos** using the AI Walkthrough Note feature effectively, step by step.

<Warning>
  **Prerequisite**: Ensure that the feature is enabled for your organization. If it is not enabled, contact our account admin or email [**support@zuper.co**](mailto:support@zuper.co) to have it enabled.
</Warning>

# Capturing Photos

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Android">
    * Open the Zuper mobile app.
    * Navigate to the Job Details page.
    * Tap **Add Note**. The camera will launch automatically.
    * Go to the **Walkthrough** option.
    * Switch to the **camera mode** at the bottom of the screen.

    <img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Ap6_gJ6y-c8B2Xtw/images/AndroidDualcamera-portrait.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Ap6_gJ6y-c8B2Xtw&q=85&s=135c48ac2dbfefad80aadbf38c5343e0" data-path="images/AndroidDualcamera-portrait.png" />

    * Click “**Start Walkthrough**” to begin the session.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="iOS">
    * Open the Zuper mobile app.
    * Navigate to the Job Details page.
    * Tap **Add Note**. The camera will launch automatically.
    * Go to the **WALKTHROUGH PHOTO** option.

    <img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/ZxQOQEn6Mgs2bwbK/images/WalkthroughphotoiOSnew-portrait.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=ZxQOQEn6Mgs2bwbK&q=85&s=9f2f233a9e844d0ce6c401bedc8ebbf8" data-path="images/WalkthroughphotoiOSnew-portrait.png" />

    * Click “**Start Walkthrough**” to begin the session.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  **Note**: You can capture multiple photos while recording audio.
</Note>

* Once you're done, your recording will be prepared for preview.
* After previewing, you’ll be directed to the **Add Note** page, where Zuper AI will automatically generate a summary of your recorded audio, improving clarity, spelling, and grammar.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/SR5Y7F9ICGyDqcYh/images/ai5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=SR5Y7F9ICGyDqcYh&q=85&s=613379a43bc0134445e8f01cad04e4bc" data-path="images/ai5.png" />

* Tap **Save**. The summarized note, along with the captured images, will be added to the job notes successfully.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/CnXsaVzX1o8zG7qq/Zuper_AI/ai6.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=CnXsaVzX1o8zG7qq&q=85&s=8e691d6dffd5ab9b9792c9ca0bd96b6b" data-path="Zuper_AI/ai6.png" />

## Editing the Note (Optional)

After AI generates the summarized note, you can modify it as needed:

* Tap on the "**Summarized by AI**" section to open the **Edit Note** screen.
* Choose from two editing options:
  1. **Microphone Input** – Use your microphone to speak and add more details to the note.
  2. **Manual Editing** – Use the keyboard to make changes to the text manually.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/CnXsaVzX1o8zG7qq/Zuper_AI/ai7.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=CnXsaVzX1o8zG7qq&q=85&s=f5a1d117a7528464e94ea6029b83bf32" data-path="Zuper_AI/ai7.png" />

## Enhancing the Note with AI

After you've edited the note, you can further refine it using the AI capabilities listed below before saving.

* **Summarize** – Condenses lengthy notes into clear, concise content.
* **Clean up** – Corrects grammar, spelling, and sentence structure.
* **Translate** – Convert notes into 17 different languages, including Dutch, French, Slovenian, and Spanish.

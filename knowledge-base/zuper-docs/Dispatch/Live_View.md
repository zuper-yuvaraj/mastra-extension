# Live View in Dispatch Board

In fast-moving field service environments, dispatchers need to react quickly to what’s happening on the ground. Yet, urgent jobs often remain hidden due to filters or outdated views.

**Live View** in Zuper changes that.

Live View offers a **real-time, filter-free stream** of all unscheduled and unassigned jobs that are due *today* or *tomorrow*. It’s designed to help dispatchers stay focused on what needs attention *right now*, without the clutter of long-term scheduling or irrelevant filters.

### **Key Highlights**:

* Displays only jobs scheduled or due within the next 48 hours.
* Supports up to 500 jobs without compromising speed or responsiveness.

<Check>
  **Prerequisite**: **Live View** is available only when the **Auto-Sync** feature is enabled for your account. If Auto-Sync is *active*, Live View is automatically ready to use.\
  If you don’t see this feature in your Dispatch Board, contact [support@zuper.co](mailto:support@zuper.co) to get it enabled.
</Check>

## **Accessing Live View**

To open Live View:

1. Click the **Scheduling and Dispatching** module from the left navigation menu and select **Dispatch Board**.

   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY/images/live1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY&q=85&s=e95acab8f16a730be668bd98bbeba5c9" alt="Live1 Pn" width="1913" height="803" data-path="images/live1.png" />
2. Select the **Jobs** stream.
3. Click the Job Type dropdown and choose **Live View**.

   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY/images/live2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY&q=85&s=b58075b730c33551c4749aa5f901a249" alt="Live2 Pn" width="1916" height="876" data-path="images/live2.png" />
4. You’ll see two tabs- **Today** and **Tomorrow.**
   * The Today tab lists all jobs due/scheduled to be dispatched today.

     <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY/images/live3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY&q=85&s=c344091e49ffb24d003bfdfafd96f18a" alt="Live3 Pn" width="1919" height="886" data-path="images/live3.png" />
   * The Tomorrow tab shows jobs that need attention for the next day.

     <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY/images/live4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY&q=85&s=d13afcbc165f6608e5fd4a46393944e2" alt="Live4 Pn" width="1913" height="881" data-path="images/live4.png" />

<Note>
  **Note**: Jobs are sequenced in the following order:

  * Scheduled Jobs are displayed first and sorted by start time.
  * Unscheduled Jobs are listed next, sorted by due date.
</Note>

As soon as a job is scheduled or assigned, it will disappear from the Live View Dispatch queue, keeping your focus sharp and up-to-date. Similarly, as new jobs are created, you see them only if they’re scheduled/due for today or tomorrow in the live view.

## **Using Live View**

Live View is designed for simplicity and speed. Once you're in Live view, you can:

* **Search for Jobs**: Type a keyword or job number in the search bar to quickly locate specific jobs.
* **Assign Jobs Instantly**: Drag a job card and drop it into a technician’s available slot in the Scheduler. Set the start and end times in the pop-up, then click **Update**.
* **Find Nearby Technicians**: Right-click a job card and select **Find Live Technicians**. Zuper highlights nearby technicians along with their distance from the job site, helping you assign the right person faster. 

### FAQs

**Q: Can I apply filters or sorting in Live View Dispatcher Queue?**\
**A**: No. Live View intentionally disables filters and custom sorts to ensure a pure, priority-driven real-time stream.

**Q: What job types are shown?**\
**A**: Only unscheduled and/or unassigned jobs that are due or scheduled within the next 48 hours.

**Will Live View affect my existing Dispatch Board view?**\
**A**: No, it runs as a separate dropdown view within the Jobs stream.

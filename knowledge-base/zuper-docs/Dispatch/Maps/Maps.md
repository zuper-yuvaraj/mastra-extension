# Accessing the Maps

The maps module in Zuper provides a panoramic view that helps you track the exact location of users, jobs, routes, assets, properties, customers, and organizations on the map. Additionally, you can perform various actions such as creating routes, adding jobs, assigning users, optimizing routes, and more directly from within this module.

## Users

To track the exact location of users on the map, follow these steps:

1. Click the "**Maps**" icon at the left navigation menu. You will land on the maps page.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH/Dispatch/Maps/M1.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH&q=85&s=258a776f92841fab2fcc799b637c0f75" alt="" width="1914" height="862" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/M1.jpg" />

2. On the maps page, click "**Users**" at the top, which lists all the users in the team (the team will be set by default).

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH/Dispatch/Maps/M2.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH&q=85&s=cc4441e37ce5d8661c08256867f1948e" alt="" width="1914" height="862" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/M2.jpg" />

3. Click any user to view their exact location on the map. You can also search for and view specific users on a team. Click the filter icon to search for a user based on the chosen team and user type (online and offline).

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/m7.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=8fe11fe3a24fc228826ebeb6ad8eae41" alt="" width="1914" height="862" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/m7.jpg" />

5. To view any user details and location history, click the "**Kebab**" icon next to the user and select "**Show Details**." You can view the user details such as their role, email ID, phone number, skill set, team, work hours, and so on data on the right.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH/Dispatch/Maps/M3.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH&q=85&s=7852624c43c3f26f4f6bf44eaa4f5e0f" alt="" width="1915" height="868" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/M3.jpg" />

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH/Dispatch/Maps/M4.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH&q=85&s=fe5484e9a2d91bcec7ef823d7dc672d0" alt="" width="1905" height="868" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/M4.jpg" />

However, if you select "**History**," you can view the user's location history on the map for the selected date.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/m9.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=69bd64813f3a52de8fa6df54a8980358" alt="" width="1915" height="868" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/m9.jpg" />

6. You can also pin/unpin users. Click the "**Pin**" icon next to the filters.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/m8.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=2acdfe536d2485adad72472584e47e4e" alt="" width="1914" height="862" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/m8.jpg" />

</Note>

## Routes

1. On the maps page, click "**Routes**" at the top, which lists all the routes created. Click any of the routes to view the exact location on the map. You can use the zoom-in and zoom-out icons to adjust the map view.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH/Dispatch/Maps/R1.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH&q=85&s=016e8d7c05db8acc165a601352a1f410" alt="" width="1901" height="869" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R1.jpg" />

2. You can also search and view specific routes on the map. Click the "**Filter**" icon to search for a route based on the date the picker selects.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/R2.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=253c388558927a717f33638a4cc94deb" alt="" width="1910" height="870" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R2.jpg" />

<Note>
  The blue dot represents the route present for the day.
</Note>

3. You can also pin/unpin a route. Click the "**Pin**" icon next to the filters.

<Note>
  **Note:** When a route is pinned, its location will appear in all other selected modules on the map. When unpinned, it will not appear in other modules.
</Note>

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/R13.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=07ccf382aa0d733c60220a2538c5e44f" alt="" width="1917" height="872" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R13.jpg" />

### Create New Route

In addition to viewing the route on the map, you can create a new route from here.

1. Click the "+" icon next to Routes.<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/R4.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=79bac514b66d625183b32ea8724856d6" alt="" width="1910" height="870" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R4.jpg" />

2. A sidebar appears on the right to create a route. Enter the following details on the page and click the "**Next**" button.

* Route Name- Provide a name for the route.
* Route Color - Choose the route color from the drop-down menu to be marked on the map.
* Mode of Transport- Choose the transport mode (Car/semi-truck, truck, pedestrian) used on the route.
* Route type - Choose the route type (Fastest travel time or shortest distance) that the technician needs to follow.
  Start Location- Pick the location from where the route would start.
  End Location- Pick the location where the route ends.
* Departure- Select the date and Time of departure.
* Duration- Enter the estimated time (in hours) that will be taken for the route to complete.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/R7.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=0efe77ebd91e396444f8b9fd3f06b5aa" alt="" width="1914" height="866" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R7.jpg" />

3. Once the route has been created, you can add a job on the following page. To do so, click "**+ Add Job**."

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/R8.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=351b9f297d0856f6b0c7c723acc74553" alt="" width="1918" height="868" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R8.jpg" />

4. Select the Jobs to be added and click the "**Choose Job**" button to add them to the route.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/R9.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=c71d39aab374a70b233eee756863aa9a" alt="" width="1918" height="873" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R9.jpg" />

<Note>
  **Note:** The jobs with an exclamation mark cannot be selected as they do not have any geo-coordinates in their route.
</Note>

5. After adding, click the "**Create**" button. The route will be created successfully.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH/Dispatch/Maps/R10.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH&q=85&s=bef98d862bfbf3d42d19cd6f7c4e1453" alt="" width="1915" height="871" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R10.jpg" />

6. Once the route has been created, you can view details and edit or clone the route by clicking the "**Kebab**" icon.

When you click "**Edit Route**," you can make necessary changes to the previously created route. Once done, click the "**Update**" button to save the changes.

When you click "**Clone Route**," The same route with the same details will be cloned and added as a new route. You can also make changes if needed.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH/Dispatch/Maps/R12.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=-S_TpUncdyLgPIzH&q=85&s=d7a96775c2f675c01d03f37f92d64f5e" alt="" width="1913" height="859" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R12.jpg" />

**Show Details**

Using the actions icons at the top left, you can lock the route, assign a user, optimize the route, and edit route/reorder jobs/clone route/delete the route by clicking the "**Kebab**" menu.

<Note>
  **Note:** When you lock the route, you won't be able to update it. This means you can't change the route location, reschedule, add more jobs to the route, or reorder them.
</Note>

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/R6.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=faac437758313348832ed3e159d77b4f" alt="" width="1915" height="873" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/R6.jpg" />

## Maps Settings

Two major settings can be applied to maps:

* **Overlay all routes on the map.**
* **Overlay service territories.**

1. Click the "**Settings**" icon at the top right corner of the map and select the following.

* **Overlay all routes on the map**- When selected, you can view all the routes on the map.
* Overlay service territories- When this is selected, you can view the territories where the routes and jobs are carried out efficiently on the map. Once selected, click the "**Save**" button to confirm.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/set1.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=ebcd41794bd07ba7d6cfffc5e3fb3a63" alt="" width="1917" height="862" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/set1.jpg" />

* To create new service territories, navigate to Settings and select Service Territory. You can create a new service territory by choosing the "**New Service Territory**" option. Additionally, it is possible to edit or deactivate existing service territories by selecting "**Edit Service Territory**" and "**Deactivate Territory**," respectively.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM/Dispatch/Maps/set2.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=p4EudAsCjgsdYPLM&q=85&s=33690e5e6b4b562180f136a1acbccbe1" alt="" width="1916" height="857" data-path="Dispatch/Maps/set2.jpg" />

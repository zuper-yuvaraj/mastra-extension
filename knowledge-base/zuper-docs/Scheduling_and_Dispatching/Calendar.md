# Accessing the Calendar/Schedule board

Zuper's powerful calendar module provides a unified view of your entire schedule, including one-time and recurring jobs, non-job events, technician time-offs, and holidays. With its intuitive interface, you can easily navigate through different time frames - from day to week to month - and gain a clear overview of your team's activities and availability.

Effortlessly drag and drop to reschedule jobs, quickly identify scheduling conflicts, and optimize your resource allocation. Color-coded entries allow for instant recognition of different event types, while customizable filters help you focus on specific teams, job types, or locations.

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Scheduling & Dispatching module* -> *Calendar*
</Frame>

# Accessing calendar

* Select the **scheduling & dispatching** module icon from the left navigation menu and choose "**Calendar**."
* The calendar will display events based on the selected view (Day, Week, or Month).

## Actions in the Calendar Module

### 1. View and schedule unscheduled Jobs

Unscheduled jobs are pending tasks that have yet to be assigned a specific time slot. To view and schedule them:

* Click the **"Unscheduled Jobs"** button at the top right corner of the page.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=3d26528ac184434157e0caa617a6f477" alt="" width="1920" height="827" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar3.png" />

* A list of unscheduled and overdue jobs will appear. Drag and drop a job onto the calendar to schedule it.

<Note>
  **Note**: When you drag and drop, a pop-up will appear as shown below, where you will be prompted to confirm the scheduled/rescheduled start & end date and time. Once done, click the "**Update**" button.
</Note>

* The job is scheduled successfully. Once scheduled, you can **reschedule, add a note, clone, or delete** it by right-clicking on it.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=c1f3355187cf0af1e57c5789318fc33e" alt="" width="1917" height="1649" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar4.png" />

### 2. Create & schedule a new Job

This feature allows you to create and schedule jobs directly from the calendar, streamlining job assignments without navigating away from the scheduling interface.

* Click and drag over the desired time slot to create a new job.
* A sidebar appears with pre-filled date and time details.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/0hbDl4NBbErqwot_/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=0hbDl4NBbErqwot_&q=85&s=0cd8c678d1bbb90284880ffc1864d8d9" alt="" width="1848" height="897" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar5.png" />

* Enter additional details such as **Job Title, Customer, Organization, Property, Category, etc.,** and click **"Save."**
* To add more details like **service tasks, parts/services, assets, contracts, and attachments,** click **"Create Detailed Job"** at the top right of the sidebar.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/0hbDl4NBbErqwot_/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar6.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=0hbDl4NBbErqwot_&q=85&s=77789836fc21f97a02b92cd7c8ff82ad" alt="" width="1852" height="1803" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar6.png" />

* Once the job is created, you can manage it by rescheduling, adding notes, cloning, or deleting it.
* You can also create a new job using the **"+ Create Job"** button at the top right corner.

<Note>
  **Note**: When you create a new job using the "**Create Job**" button at the top right corner. You will then need to follow the steps mentioned above but remember that the date and time won't be prefilled since you are creating a new job from scratch.
</Note>

### 3. Create and review timeoff requests

Manage technician availability effectively by adding time-off requests directly in the calendar. This feature helps plan workloads efficiently while ensuring seamless scheduling.

To create a new time-off request from the calendar, follow these steps:

* Click the **drop-down menu** next to "Create Job" and select **"New Time-off."**

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/0hbDl4NBbErqwot_/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar8.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=0hbDl4NBbErqwot_&q=85&s=05bda2e3c61d252a433d376766e9e0da" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar8.png" />

* Enter details such as **Start & End Date, User, Team, Reason (Sick, Vacation, etc.), and Remarks.**
* Check **"Notify User"** if you want to send a notification about the request.
* Click **"Submit"** to create the time-off request.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/0hbDl4NBbErqwot_/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar9.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=0hbDl4NBbErqwot_&q=85&s=46b3e547fb17aa29a6adfff3dba5687a" alt="" width="1920" height="829" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar9.png" />

* After creating a time-off request, you can view more information by hovering over the request. To edit the request, simply click on it.
* Ensure that the **"Display Time-off"** checkbox is enabled to view submitted requests in the calendar.

### 4. Create and review non-job events

A **non-job event** refers to any activity that a field technician or employee participates in that is **not directly linked to a specific job**. These events can include meetings, training sessions, administrative tasks, or any other non-job-related activities. To create a new non-job event from the calendar,

Click the drop-down menu next to "Create Job" and select "**New Non Job Event**."

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar10.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=aff86f0fbf1061ea8eec73275b51410c" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar10.png" />

By creating a non-job event in the calendar, dispatchers and managers are informed that the technician will be **unavailable for job assignments** during that period.

For step-by-step instructions on how to create a non-job event, see creating and managing a non-job event article.

### 5. Calendar Sidebar

The sidebar provides quick access to the scheduled jobs in the calendar. Here's a breakdown of the components found in the sidebar:

    The mini calendar displays all dates in a month, allowing you to navigate through different days, weeks, and months easily. It also serves as a visual representation of scheduled jobs on the date.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Filters" defaultOpen={false}>
    The filter feature allows you to search scheduled jobs in the calendar by **category, status, team,** and **user**. To remove the filters applied, click the “**Reset**” button next to the filters.

    <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar12.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=737aa37da437917c389302f1d2965aeb" alt="" width="1848" height="897" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar12.png" />
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Advanced filter" defaultOpen={false}>
    Advanced filters feature allows you to quickly view jobs in the calendar by applying various filters. This means, if you cannot locate the specific job using the above-said parameters, you can use this option for a more refined search:

    <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar13.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=869765dc5f3edb33b44541deb3a8bc6d" alt="" width="1859" height="906" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar13.png" />

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Step 1">
        Click the “**Advanced Filters**” option at the bottom of the sidebar. This will open a “Calendar Filter” at the right.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Step 2">
        Apply filters such as **Job Type, Category, Status, Due Date, Customer, Organization, or Team.**
      </Step>

      <Step title="Step 3">
        Click **"Apply"** to update the view.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Step 4">
        To save the applied filter, check **"Save as Filter."** Saved filters appear in **"Quick Filter"** for easy access.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Step 5">

### 6. Calendar settings

The settings in the calendar module allow you to customize its display and job card details for a better user experience.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Job card information" defaultOpen={false}>
    To customize job cards displayed in the calendar:

    * Click the **"Settings"** icon at the top right corner.

          <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar15.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=4f00d108b111a7e009b19f0223870c5f" alt="" width="1852" height="905" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar15.png" />

    * Select **"Job Card Information."**

    * Choose a primary and secondary color for job categories.

          <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar16.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=a42d31d15fecca52f24acd59831ea125" alt="" width="1848" height="897" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar16.png" />

    * Customize displayed attributes for **Week & Day View** and **Month View.**

    * Click **"Save"** to apply changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Display" defaultOpen={false}>
    * Click **"Settings"** at the top right corner.

    * Select **"Display."**

          <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar17.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=6b37e2d186dc20d523745847e801cf20" alt="" width="1848" height="897" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar17.png" />

    * Set **the start of the week** (e.g., Monday, Sunday).

    * Choose the **default view** (Day, Week, or Month).

          <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN/Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar18.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=4-Los4YX_ob2IgRN&q=85&s=5155c55bd951a974b0077b478973890a" alt="" width="1848" height="897" data-path="Scheduling_and_Dispatching/calendar18.png" />

    * Enable/disable additional options:
      1. **Business Hours** - When enabled, calendar shows only the business hours and does not show out-of-business hours to schedule a job.
      2. **Weekends**- When enabled, the weekend (Saturday and Sunday) is also included while scheduling a job.
      3. **Week Numbers**: When enabled, week numbers are included in the calendar to schedule a job.
      4. **Display Time-off** - Show time-off requests.

    * Click **"Save"** to apply settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Troubleshooting

<Warning>
  You can use this table to find out why a job cannot be rescheduled and what to do next.
</Warning>

| Cause | What to do |
| - | - |
| Job is already completed or canceled | Ask your admin to reopen the job, then try again. |
| You don't have permission to edit jobs | Ask your admin to update your access or make the change for you. |
| The new time is outside working hours | Pick a time within your business hours, or ask your admin to update the working hours in **Settings**. |
| This is part of a repeating job | Open the job directly and edit the time from there. |
| Something else is blocking the change | Contact [Zuper Support](mailto:support@zuper.co) with a description of the job and what you were trying to do. |

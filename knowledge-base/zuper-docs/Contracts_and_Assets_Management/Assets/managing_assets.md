# Managing your assets

Efficient asset management ensures operational efficiency and extends equipment lifespan. Zuper’s unified platform enables seamless asset creation, tracking, and management. With centralized control, businesses can monitor asset status, track history, and streamline maintenance.

This guide covers accessing the Assets listing page, key actions, and managing asset details effectively.

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Contracts and Assets Management module -> Assets-> Assets listing page -> Asset Details*  
</Frame>

## Accessing the assets listing page

The asset listing page is designed to help you stay organized by providing a centralized view of all assets and enabling quick access to critical asset details. The page includes several key features to streamline asset management:

## Listing View

Customize the Assets listing by adding/removing or reordering columns, then Update View to overwrite or save it as a new view.

**Update View**

Click Update View to modify the current view after changes.

**Save as a new view**

Use the dropdown beside Update View → Save as new view.

**Create View dialog**

Enter the View Name (mandatory), choose Share with (User/Team) and add users, toggle Visibility to all users, and click Create.

**Reset View**

Click Reset View to restore the current view to the default.

**Permissions**

* **Edit**: Add/remove/reorder columns; save as new or overwrite existing views.
* **View-only**: Apply views but can’t edit; can Save as new view to copy without altering the original.
* **Manage view**: Rename, adjust Visibility (Only Me/User/Team or global toggle), Duplicate, or Delete (creator only; reverts to default/another saved view).

## Pinned filters

Zuper's Assets module lets you use pinned filters to streamline your filter experience. Pinned filters keep your most-used criteria readily accessible for quick application.

1. **Pin Filters for Quick Access**

* Once your filters are set, click the **Pin Filters** button in the dialog box to save them as pinned.
* Pinned filters appear in the dialog box's "**Pinned Filters**" section, allowing you to apply them with one click in future sessions.

### Bulk Action:

The bulk printing of QR codes on the Assets is possible. With this, Assets can get their QR code printed in size.

 1. Select the list of assets you want to bulk update and choose the option “**Print QR Code.**”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/1Dzz6aS8fECz91-C/images/AV2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=1Dzz6aS8fECz91-C&q=85&s=21489b5c42b625c47dffed548a07fcda" alt="AV2 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/AV2.png" />

2. The QR Codes are successfully printed in bulk.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC/images/Ast2.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC&q=85&s=e72a7b6abd6137f500fae6bb1040adec" alt="Ast2 Jp" width="1883" height="833" data-path="images/Ast2.jpg" />

<Note>
  **Note:** The Bulk Print QR code is available for less than **100** assets at a time; if more than **100** assets are selected, the PDF is generated and sent over email. We can print the QR code using any printer. 
</Note>

**Compatibility:**

<Check>
  **The exported PDF will print to 1” x 2 5/8” sized labels—this format prints on the Avery Template Presta 94200 template.**
</Check>

With this new feature, businesses can save time by updating the asset status and asset fields for multiple assets with a single click.

The assets will have a QR code for identification for internal purposes, and a list of QR codes will be sent to field executives during field service. This bulk option helps them print multiple QR codes easily.

## Managing Asset Details 

Once an asset is created or accessed from the listing page, the **Asset Details Page** becomes your central hub for managing its lifecycle. The page follows a three-column layout, the left panel displays the asset's primary details, while the right panel provides contextual insights and quick actions for efficient asset management.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/N0-kTgRb79_nCoAC/images/asset38.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=N0-kTgRb79_nCoAC&q=85&s=fb69b7df1d313d62b3b0c5ef8e9c8af4" alt="Asset38 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/asset38.png" />

## Left panel 

The left panel contains asset-related details such as the asset name and code. Below this, you will find the quick actions bar, which provides contextual action buttons, including options to view the history of performed actions, create a new job or project, add notes, and view the asset's QR code (which can be printed and affixed to the physical asset for tracking and identification purposes).

From here, you also have quick access to view information such as asset details, associated parts, notes, history, activity, and jobs.

## Right panel

In the right panel, view the organization or contact information associated with the asset, and manage its links to various modules, such as properties, projects, contracts, organizations, PPM, and more. To associate a module, click the “**+**” icon next to the desired module.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/N0-kTgRb79_nCoAC/images/asset42.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=N0-kTgRb79_nCoAC&q=85&s=1e7045afdcdba938ea15b09a05028950" alt="Asset42 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/asset42.png" />

## More actions   

Managing assets doesn’t stop at viewing details—you can perform various actions such as editing, cloning, printing, sharing, and deactivating the asset. To do this, click the "**More Actions**" button at the top right corner of the page and select the desired option. 

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/N0-kTgRb79_nCoAC/images/asset43.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=N0-kTgRb79_nCoAC&q=85&s=5d608c99d4b83045abda57f2fbf6b34a" alt="Asset43 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/asset43.png" />

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Edit Asset " defaultOpen="false">
    Use the **Edit Asset** option to modify existing asset details as needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clone " defaultOpen="false">
    Use the **Clone** **Asset option** to quickly create a replica of an existing asset, saving time. The current asset details will be duplicated onto a new asset page, where you can make any necessary edits before saving. 
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Print/Save as PDF " defaultOpen="false">
    Use this option to print the asset details or save them as a PDF for documentation purposes. 
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Share via Email " defaultOpen="false">
    This option enables you to share asset details via email directly from the platform. 
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Deactivate" defaultOpen="false">
    Use the Deactivate option to disable the asset. Once deactivated, you will have the option to **delete** the asset, if needed. 
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Managing assets efficiently is crucial for ensuring their optimal usage, tracking their status, and maintaining accurate records. With Zuper, you have a centralized platform to oversee all asset-related activities—from creation and modification to tracking history, managing associated parts, and linking jobs or projects.

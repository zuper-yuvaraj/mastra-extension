# Overview

Zuper’s Assets Module streamlines asset management by enabling businesses to track, maintain, and optimize their physical assets efficiently. By centralizing asset data, businesses can minimize downtime, extend equipment lifespan, and improve service delivery.

With features like preventive maintenance scheduling, asset history tracking, and seamless association with jobs, contracts, and customers, Zuper ensures peak operational efficiency—all within a unified platform.

### **Key Benefits of the Assets Module**

<Icon icon="cubes" color="black" /> **Comprehensive Asset Tracking**
Easily track and manage all assets in a centralized system. Record essential details such as asset codes, serial numbers, purchase dates, warranty details, and current status for complete visibility and control.

<Icon icon="calendar-clock" color="Black" /> **Preventive Maintenance Scheduling**
Plan and schedule **preventive maintenance (PPM)** to keep assets in optimal condition, reducing unexpected breakdowns and minimizing downtime.

<Icon icon="link" color="Black" /> **Seamless Asset Associations**
Link assets with **contracts, customers, jobs, projects, and properties**, creating a structured asset management workflow that enhances operational efficiency.

<Icon icon="tag" color="Black" /> **Parts & Services Management**
Track parts usage and service requirements for each asset to ensure timely repairs and maintenance. Maintain inventory control and prevent service delays due to missing components.

<Icon icon="clock-rotate-left" color="Black" /> **Detailed Asset History**  Access a complete history of each asset, including maintenance activities, service records, and repair logs, to gain insights into asset performance and make informed decisions about replacements or upgrades.

<Icon icon="arrow-up-from-bracket" color="black" /> **Custom Attributes & Reporting**
Customize asset attributes based on your business needs and generate detailed reports on asset performance, maintenance schedules, and key operational metrics to drive data-driven decision-making.

# Connect a Supplier

> Order roofing materials from SRS Distribution or ABC Supply without leaving Zuper.

***Stop switching between systems to price and order materials.***

Ordering roofing materials often means leaving Zuper to check pricing elsewhere, then returning to update the job. This slow back-and-forth can cost you accuracy and time on every estimate.

Zuper connects directly to your account with SRS Distribution or ABC Supply. Once connected, live branch pricing flows straight into your estimates and material orders. Zuper refreshes this pricing every day, so your team always quotes accurate numbers.

When an order ships, delivery updates and the supplier invoice appear right on the material order. This gives your team one place to track every material from quote to delivery.

Ready to get started? If you order from SRS Distribution, see [Connect your SRS account](https://docs.zuper.co/Integrations/Purchasing/SRS-setup) to link your account.

If you order from ABC Supply, see [Connecting ABC Supply](https://docs.zuper.co/Integrations/Purchasing/abc-supply/connecting-abc-supply) to link your account instead.

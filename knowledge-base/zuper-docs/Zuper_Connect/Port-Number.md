# Porting Phone Numbers to Zuper Connect

Number porting allows you to transfer your existing business phone numbers from your current telecom provider to Zuper Connect without changing the numbers your customers already use.

Once the porting process is completed:

* Calls and SMS messages are handled through **Zuper Connect**, allowing your team to manage customer communication directly from the platform while continuing to use the same business phone numbers.
* The ported numbers can use Zuper Connect features such as **AI-powered interactions**, **call routing**, and **communication automation**, helping streamline customer communication and workflows.

The Zuper Connect team manages the porting process and coordinates with the telecom carrier to complete the transfer.

## Quick Summary

The following table provides a quick overview of the phone number porting process in Zuper Connect, including the typical timeline, requirements, and actions needed from your organization.

| **Item** | **Details** |
| :- | :- |
| Managed by | Zuper Connect Team |
| Typical Porting Time | 2 days – 4 weeks |
| SMS Requirement | [10DLC](/Zuper_Connect/10DLC#what-is-10dlc-and-why-should-you-care) approval is required before porting |
| Customer Action | Submit the porting request form. |
| Downtime | Minimal |

## When to Use This

You may need to port phone numbers to Zuper Connect in the following situations:

* Your business already has phone numbers that customers use to contact you.
* You want to move your communication system from another telecom or VoIP provider to Zuper Connect.
* You want to manage calls and SMS messages directly within Zuper Connect.
* You want to use Zuper Connect features such as call routing, automation, or AI-powered communication while keeping your existing numbers.

If you do not have existing phone numbers, you can request new numbers directly through Zuper Connect instead of porting.

## How Phone Number Porting Works

Porting a phone number involves several stages, where the information is verified and approved by the current carrier.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete 10DLC Registration (For SMS)">
    If your organization intends to send SMS messages using the ported numbers, [10DLC registration](/Zuper_Connect/10DLC#step-1-gather-your-business-information) must be completed first. This registration ensures compliance with telecom regulations and allows SMS messaging to function correctly after the port.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the Zuper Connect Porting Form">
    The Zuper Connect team will provide a **Porting Request Form** to start the process.

    The form requires the following details:

    * Phone numbers to port
    * Current carrier name
    * Account number
    * Port-out PIN
    * Business name exactly as listed with the carrier
    * Service address
    * Recent phone bill
    * Signed Letter of Authorization (LOA)

    Providing accurate information helps prevent delays during carrier verification.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zuper Connect Submits the Port Request">
    After the form is submitted, the Zuper Connect team reviews the provided information and submits the porting request to the telecom carrier.

    The carrier then validates the request against the information on their records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Carrier Validation">
    The current carrier verifies several details before approving the port request. These include:

    * Account ownership
    * Business information
    * Phone number status
    * Any pending changes or restrictions on the account

    If the request is approved, the carrier issues a **Firm Order Commitment (FOC) date** for the transfer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Port Completion">
    On the scheduled **FOC date**:

    * The phone numbers are transferred to Zuper Connect.
    * Calls and SMS services are activated within the platform.
    * The numbers are configured and ready for use in the Zuper Connect account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Typical Porting Timelines

The time required to port a number depends on the type of number and the carrier involved.

| **Number Type** | **Typical Timeline** |
| :- | :- |
| Toll-Free Numbers | 2–10 business days |
| VoIP Numbers | 1–3 weeks |
| Fixed Landline Numbers | 2–4 weeks |
| Bulk Ports | Depends on the number of phone numbers |

Timelines may vary depending on carrier validation and account configuration.

### Supported Number Types

Zuper Connect supports porting multiple types of phone numbers.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Local Numbers">
    Local numbers are standard 10-digit business phone numbers associated with a geographic region.

    Example:

    (405) 555-1234\
    (512) 555-9876
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Toll-Free Numbers">
    Supported toll-free prefixes include:

    * 800
    * 833
    * 844
    * 855
    * 866
    * 877
    * 888
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="VoIP Numbers">
    Numbers hosted by internet-based phone systems can be ported from providers such as:

    * RingCentral
    * Dialpad
    * Aircall
    * Grasshopper
    * Nextiva
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fixed Landline Numbers">
    Traditional telecom numbers from carriers such as AT\&T, Verizon, Comcast, and CenturyLink can also be ported.

    These ports may take slightly longer due to additional carrier processing requirements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Special Cases">
    **Google Voice Numbers**\
    Google Voice numbers can be ported, but they must first be unlocked in the Google Voice account settings before submitting the port request.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my number during porting?">
    Yes, your phone number continues to work with your current carrier until the porting process is complete.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I cancel my current phone service?">
    No, do not cancel your current phone service. Canceling your service might release the phone number and prevent it from being ported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can fixed landline numbers be ported?">
    Yes, fixed landline numbers can be ported. However, they might take longer to transfer compared to VoIP numbers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I port multiple numbers?">
    Yes, you can port multiple phone numbers in a single request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What information is required to submit a port request?">
    To process a phone number porting request, Zuper Connect might require the following information:

    * Business name, exactly as registered with your current carrier
    * Service address on file with your current carrier
    * List of phone numbers to be ported
    * Current telecom provider name
    * A recent phone bill or Customer Service Record (CSR)
    * An authorized contact who can approve the port request

    The Zuper Connect team collects this information through the porting request form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to sign a Letter of Authorization?">
    Yes, most telecom carriers require a Letter of Authorization (LOA) to approve the number transfer. The LOA allows Zuper Connect to request the number transfer from your current provider on your behalf.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What types of phone numbers can be ported to Zuper Connect?">
    The following types of numbers can usually be ported:

    * Local phone numbers
    * Toll-free numbers
    * Business landline numbers
    * VoIP numbers, including numbers from services such as Google Voice

    Eligibility for VoIP numbers might depend on carrier approval.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does number porting typically take?">
    Porting timelines might vary depending on the number type and your current carrier. Typical timelines include the following:

    * Toll-free numbers: two to five business days
    * Local VoIP numbers: five to 10 business days
    * Landline numbers: up to two to four weeks

    Actual timelines might vary depending on carrier validation and approval.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there any requirements before I submit a port request?">
    Yes, confirm the following before you submit a port request, to avoid delays or rejection:

    * Your phone numbers remain active with your current provider throughout the porting process.
    * Your business name and service address match your carrier's records.
    * All porting details are accurate and complete.

    Incorrect or mismatched information might delay your port request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why might a port request be rejected?">
    A port request might be rejected for several reasons, including the following:

    * A mismatch between the submitted information and your carrier's records
    * Incorrect account details or service address
    * Phone numbers that are inactive or disconnected
    * An unauthorized port request
    * Carrier restrictions on certain numbers

    If Zuper Connect rejects a port request, the Zuper Connect team reviews the reason and guides you through the next steps to resubmit your request.

    If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

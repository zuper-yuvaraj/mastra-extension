# Connect Dialer in HubSpot

## **Overview**

The Zuper Connect dialer works seamlessly inside HubSpot, so you can make and receive calls without switching apps. This lets you stay focused on your contacts, deals, and other HubSpot tasks while handling calls.

Zuper Connect – HubSpot Integration: [Call Activities Sync](https://docs.zuper.co/Zuper_Connect/Zuperconnect-HubSpot-Integration)

## **Key Capabilities**

* Make calls to customers or other Zuper Connect agents.
* Receive incoming calls with alerts.
* Check your recent call history.
* Initiate calls directly from customer or user profiles within HubSpot.
* Use basic call tools like **hold**, **mute**, **transfer**, **warm**, and **cold** call transfers.

## **Prerequisites**

Before starting, ensure these are set up:

1. **HubSpot Account**: You need an active HubSpot account.
2. **Zuper-HubSpot Integration**: Connect your [Zuper account to HubSpot](https://docs.zuper.co/Integrations/CRM/Zuper_HubSpot_Integration).
3. **Active Zuper Connect Subscription**: You must have a valid subscription.

## **Best Practices: Window Management**

### **Understanding the Window Setup**

The Zuper Connect dialer runs in a **separate browser window** from HubSpot. This allows you to:

* Keep the dialer visible while working in HubSpot.
* Receive call notifications while multitasking.
* Manage calls independently of HubSpot navigation.

1. You will hear an audio notification.
2. The dialer window does **not** automatically come into focus.

* Keep the dialer window open during working hours
* Set a default phone number
* Ensure your status is **Available** when ready to receive calls

Avoid logging into Zuper Connect on multiple platforms at the same time, and you will be able to use it only on one screen with the same login.

## **Troubleshooting**

### **Cannot see the Dialer Icon**

* Confirm Zuper Connect is integrated with HubSpot
* Check user permissions
* Refresh HubSpot
* Clear browser cache

### **Login Issues**

* Verify company name, email, and password
* Ensure Zuper Connect subscription is active
* Contact your admin if credentials are forgotten

### **Unable to Make or Receive Calls**

### **One Call at a Time Limitation**

You can only be active on **one call at a time** across all Zuper Connect instances.

**Scenario**

* If you are on a call in the native Zuper app, the HubSpot dialer cannot be used
* You may see: *"You are on another call."*

<Frame>
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/v-HZsqq0pMqMbVaA/images/ZHCO17.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=v-HZsqq0pMqMbVaA&q=85&s=f32d428ad71a8ea5bfff8ec6771e168d" alt="ZHCO17" width="1615" height="727" data-path="images/ZHCO17.png" />
</Frame>

### **Solution**

* End the active call first.
* Use only one Zuper Connect instance at a time.

 **Dialer Window Not Responding**

* Close and reopen the dialer.
* Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R).
* Try a different browser.
* Check internet connectivity.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the Zuper Connect Dialer, and what are its main features in HubSpot?">
    The **Zuper Connect Dialer** is a calling tool built directly into HubSpot, so you can make and receive calls without switching applications. It includes outgoing calls to customers or internal Zuper users, incoming call notifications, recent call history, direct dialing from contact and deal records, and in-call controls such as hold, mute, transfer, and conference.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I need before I can use the Zuper Connect Dialer in HubSpot?">
    You need the following:

    * An active HubSpot account, on any plan.
    * A Zuper account connected to HubSpot.
    * An active **Zuper Connect** subscription.

    If your subscription is inactive, you see a **Contact Support** prompt during login.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I log in to the Zuper Connect Dialer from HubSpot?">
    Follow these steps to log in:

    1. Select the **HubSpot dialer** icon, and choose **Zuper Connect**.
    2. Enter your **Company Name**.
    3. Enter your **Email Address**.
    4. Enter your **Password**.
    5. Select **Login**.

    Zuper Connect takes you directly to the dialer interface.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I open the Zuper Connect Dialer from inside HubSpot?">
    Follow these steps to open the dialer:

    1. Select the **HubSpot dialer** icon to open the **Zuper Connect** dialog box.
    2. Open the widget.
    3. Select **Open calling window**.

    A new browser window launches for login. The **Open calling window** button appears only when the dialer is closed, to prevent multiple windows from opening.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I control whether I receive incoming calls?">
    Set your status to control incoming calls:

    * **Available** (green): you are ready to receive calls.
    * **Unavailable**: incoming calls do not come through.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I make an outgoing call using the dialer?">
    Follow these steps to make a call:

    1. Go to the **Dialer** tab.
    2. Select your country code.
    3. Enter the number on the dial pad.
    4. Select the **Call** icon.

    Use backspace to correct mistakes on the dial pad.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the best practices for avoiding missed calls and working efficiently?">
    Follow these best practices:

    * Use split-screen view, and snap the dialer to one side with HubSpot on the other.
    * Keep the dialer open during working hours.
    * Set a default phone number.
    * Stay **Available** when you are ready to take calls.
    * Never log in with the same account on multiple devices at the same time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can I not make or receive calls, and what does 'You are on another call' mean?">
    You can be on only one active call at a time across all Zuper Connect applications. If you are on a call in the native Zuper app, the HubSpot dialer is blocked, and you see the message **You are on another call**.

    To fix this, end the other call first, or use separate user accounts for each application.

    If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I check if the dialer icon is missing or I cannot log in?">
    If the dialer icon does not appear, follow these steps:

    1. Confirm that the integration is active.
    2. Confirm that your permissions are correct.
    3. Refresh HubSpot.
    4. Clear your browser cache.

    If you cannot log in, double-check your company name, email address, and password, and confirm that your subscription is active. Contact your administrator if you forgot your credentials.

    If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I do if the dialer window is frozen or not responding?">
    Follow these steps:

    1. Close the window, and reopen it.
    2. Hard refresh your browser (Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows, or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac).
    3. Try a different browser.
    4. Check your internet connection.

    If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I access settings or log out?">
    To access settings, select the settings icon in the top bar.

    To log out, select **Logout**, and then select **Confirm**. Zuper Connect returns you to the login screen.
  </Accordion>

# Call Transcript

Zuper Connect now supports **AI-powered Call Transcription**, enabling your team to quickly review conversations, improve service quality, and ensure nothing important gets missed. Once a call is recorded, Zuper AI automatically transcribes the conversation, making it easy to scan, search, and analyze interactions with your customers.

<Note>
  **Note:** This feature is available only on select Zuper plans. To enable it for your account, please contact your account administrator or email us at [support@zuper.co](mailto:support@zuper.co)
</Note>

# Accessing Call Transcription

You can access the **AI-generated call transcript** in two ways:

## From the Conversation Thread

* Go to **Zuper Connect > Conversations Listing** from the left navigation menu.

# Call Transcription Functionality

Once inside the **Call Transcription** screen, you’ll find several tools designed to help you efficiently review and navigate the transcript:

1. **Speaker Classification**: The transcription is organized by speaker, allowing for clear and structured dialogue review.
2. **Search**: Use the **search icon** to quickly find specific words or phrases within the transcript.
3. **Copy**: Click the **copy icon** to easily copy sections of the transcript.
4. **Jump Back/Forward**: Rewind or skip audio in **10-second increments**.
5. **Play/Pause**: Control playback with simple play and pause options.
6. **Playback Speed**: Adjust the playback speed—choose from **0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, or 2x**.
7. **Transcript Audio Navigation**: Hover over any line of text to see a **play button**—click it to start audio from that point.
8. **Drag Audio Bar**: Scrub through the call using the **progress bar** to hear audio at any point.
9. **Feedback Prompt**: A quick question—*"**How is the transcript?**"*—lets you provide feedback to help improve transcription quality.

**Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

1. **Are call transcripts available for all calls?**

No, transcripts are only available for calls that are **recorded** in Zuper Connect.

2. **Can I download or export the transcript?**

Currently, transcripts can be copied using the copy icon. Export options may be considered in future updates.

3. **Are transcripts 100% accurate?**

Transcripts are AI-generated and typically accurate, but may vary based on background noise, speaker accents, or call quality.

4. **Will private or sensitive information be transcribed?**

Zuper AI is designed to exclude sensitive information like passwords or PINs during transcription.

5. **Can I provide feedback on the transcript?**

Yes! Use the built-in **"How is the transcript?"** prompt to rate and improve the feature.

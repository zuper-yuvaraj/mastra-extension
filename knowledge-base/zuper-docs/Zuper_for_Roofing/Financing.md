# Custom financing configuration

Add your own financing plans! Take any financial provider you are registered with and add your plans to your financing library.\
\
Financing plan options will be included in your proposal, allowing your customer to compare upfront payments with financed payments.

<Frame>
  1. Go to **Settings module**  → **Quotes & Invoice** → **Zuper IQ - Intelligent Quoting** → **Financing Options**.
</Frame>

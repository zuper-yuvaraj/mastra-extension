# Standard Proposal Template

Use **Standard Proposal Templates** when your roofing services follow a predictable scope and pricing structure. They are ideal for jobs where the work is clearly defined after inspection and does not require complex configuration or pricing logic.

Standard Proposal Templates help you create clean, professional proposals using predefined **service packages** and a consistent layout. They focus on clearly presenting services, pricing, and terms, making proposals easy for customers to review and approve.

To understand how Standard Proposal Templates work and how to set them up in Zuper, refer to [Standard Proposal Template](/Settings/Modules/Quotes-Invoices/Standard_proposal_template).

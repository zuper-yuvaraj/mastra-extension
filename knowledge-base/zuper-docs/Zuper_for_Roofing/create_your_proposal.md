# Build and Sign Proposal

> Description of your new file.

## Overview

Creating proposals in Zuper helps roofing businesses transform inspection findings into actionable service offerings. The Proposal feature enables teams to prepare accurate, professional options directly from inspection jobs, ensuring that customers receive a clear scope of work, pricing, and terms without delays.

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: Create Proposal from a Job: Jobs -->Job --> +(Left Panel) --> New Proposal
</Frame>

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: New Proposal: Accounting --> Quotes --> Proposal
</Frame>

1.     From the Inspection Job Details page, click "**+**" and select "**New Proposal**".

3. A new Proposal Creation page will appear. Select either "**Customer**" or "**Organization**" to associate with the proposal.

   • Expiry Date
   • Quote Template
   Click the Save button to save the proposal and proceed with sending it.

## Frequently asked questions

1. **What if the job data and the configured CPQ template do not match?** 
   If the job data and the CPQ template configuration are mismatched, you may see the **“Missing Required Job Fields”** warning in your proposal preview.
2. **What happens if a pricelist is assigned to the proposal?**
   When you assign a pricelist to a CPQ proposal, the pricelist's pricing rules apply normally to all line items by default.
   However, if a line item has an **Adjusted Markup** defined either directly on the line item or through a conditional rule, that line item is automatically excluded from price list recalculations.<br />This preserves your custom markup changes and gives them priority, while the pricelist continues to control pricing for all other unchanged items.

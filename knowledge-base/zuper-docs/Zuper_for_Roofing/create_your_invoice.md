# Create Your Invoice

**Overview**

Zuper makes invoicing easy for roofing companies. Create professional bills linked to your jobs, quotes, or properties. This guide covers converting proposals to invoices or building them from scratch after inspections, helping you bill accurately for materials, labor, and services.

An invoice requests payment for your roofing work. It lists services such as inspections and replacements, materials like shingles and nails, labor costs, taxes, and discounts. Use invoices to track project revenue, manage material payments, and maintain accurate records with customers.

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: Accounting --> Quotes  -->  More Actions --> Convert to Invoice
</Frame>

1. Select **Accounting** from the left navigation menu and select **Quotes**. . <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz/images/Invoice.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz&q=85&s=e374534354578998b01a107d0f5e2872" alt="Invoice" title="Invoice" width="1893" height="858" data-path="images/Invoice.png" />

2. On the quote details page, select **More Actions > Convert to Invoice**.
   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz/images/invoice1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz&q=85&s=43ee56cba295a6f69a2db1c8b4e3a4c2" alt="Invoice1" width="1920" height="879" data-path="images/invoice1.png" />

3. The **Invoice** creation page opens with the details prefilled from the quote. Review the invoice details and make edits as needed.

4. Select **Save as Draft** to save the invoice.
   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz/images/invoice2-1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz&q=85&s=fdc864a21bca9df0de6ce799ced8a8e9" alt="Invoice2 1" width="1920" height="864" data-path="images/invoice2-1.png" />

5. The invoice is created successfully. Select **Send** to email the invoice to the customer.
   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz/images/invoice3-1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz&q=85&s=af5690e5e0fe4ae297ad1462ff01281c" alt="Invoice3 1" width="1919" height="868" data-path="images/invoice3-1.png" />

6. The customer receives the invoice by email and can proceed with the payment.  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/PFSHDLwxhpWSR0Ls/images/invoice8.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=PFSHDLwxhpWSR0Ls&q=85&s=79b0a206caf3d0e60716424e2ba18ca5" alt="Invoice8" title="Invoice8" width="1985" height="792" data-path="images/invoice8.png" /><img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/PFSHDLwxhpWSR0Ls/images/invoice8.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=PFSHDLwxhpWSR0Ls&q=85&s=79b0a206caf3d0e60716424e2ba18ca5" alt="Invoice8" title="Invoice8" width="1985" height="792" data-path="images/invoice8.png" />

7. For an offline payment, select **More Actions > Record Payment** on the quote details page.
   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz/images/invoice6.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz&q=85&s=275bd4c17f9a944a02af188201fb0114" alt="Invoice6" width="1920" height="877" data-path="images/invoice6.png" />

8. Enter the payment details and select **Mark as Paid**.
   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz/Zuper_for_Roofing/invoice5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz&q=85&s=edee6766baf62f64eab1c9edd90a6e17" alt="Invoice5" width="1903" height="868" data-path="Zuper_for_Roofing/invoice5.png" />

9. The invoice payment is successful.
   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz/images/invoice7.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Hx-JueNyXwJyPCaz&q=85&s=6fcb79ccc6a5f556ea6b146b74b6c2d8" alt="Invoice7" width="1918" height="871" data-path="images/invoice7.png" />

With Zuper's intuitive invoicing tools, creating and managing bills for your roofing projects has never been simpler or more efficient. From converting accepted proposals to detailed breakdowns of shingles, underlayment, and labor costs, you will spend less time on paperwork and more time on what matters and delivering top-notch roofs that protect homes and build lasting customer trust.

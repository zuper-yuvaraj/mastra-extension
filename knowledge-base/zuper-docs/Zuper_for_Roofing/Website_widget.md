# Overview

> Turn your website into a 24/7 Lead Engine

A homeowner has a leak, wants to know what a new roof might cost, or is ready to book an inspection. Instead of waiting for someone to answer the phone, Website Widgets let them take the next step directly from your website.

Give homeowners a way to get an estimate, book a visit, or tell you what they need, while Zuper handles the intake behind the scenes.

When a homeowner submits a response, Zuper captures their information and creates the contact and property in your account. You can also choose to have Zuper create a job, so the opportunity is ready for your team to work on without re-entering the details.

## Put your website to work after hours

Your team doesn't have to be online for homeowners to get started. Widgets are available 24/7, so a request submitted after hours can already be waiting in Zuper when your team starts the next day.

Once your widget is ready, share it using a direct link, QR code, or embed it on your website.

Ready to get started? See [Create a Widget](/Create-a-Widget) to build your first experience.

# Get Paid

> Collect payment from the job site with the Zuper app

> Collect payment before you leave the job.

Chasing payment after a job is finished costs time and slows down cash flow. Roofing crews need a way to collect payment before they leave the site.

The Zuper mobile app lets you accept card payments directly from the job. A customer can tap a card, insert a card reader, or pay with a digital wallet.

Every payment you collect on-site syncs automatically to the invoice and updates your accounts receivable in real time.

Ready to get started? See the [Zuper Pay overview](https://docs.zuper.co/Zuper-pay/Overview) to learn how to set up and accept payments from the field.

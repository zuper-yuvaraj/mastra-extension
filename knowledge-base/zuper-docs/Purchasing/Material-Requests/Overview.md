# Overview

In the field service business, delays often occur when technicians discover that essential parts or materials are missing, damaged, or insufficient while performing a job. Communicating these needs through calls, emails, or chat messages can lead to miscommunication, a lack of traceability, and project delays. To address this challenge, Zuper introduced the **Material Request (MR)** feature, a structured, trackable way for teams to request and fulfill required materials efficiently.

The **Material Request (MR)** feature enables your organization to streamline the process of requesting, approving, and fulfilling parts and products needed by the field technician. It ensures that when a technician encounters missing, damaged, or additional material requirements during job execution, those requirements can be captured, tracked, and fulfilled efficiently either through internal stock transfer or vendor purchase order.

## Prerequisite

This feature is available only on select Zuper plans. To enable it for your account, please contact your account administrator or email us at [**support@zuper.co**](mailto:support@zuper.co)

## Key Benefits of Zuper’s Material Request Feature

Zuper’s **Material Request** feature offers several key benefits that enhance coordination between field, inventory, and procurement teams, ensuring materials are requested, approved, and delivered efficiently.

1. **Streamlined Material Planning**

Create and manage material requests directly from Jobs or Quotes. This ensures field technicians and back-office teams can communicate material needs instantly and avoid project delays due to unavailable parts.

2. **Centralized Request Tracking**

Track every material request from creation to fulfillment. View request history, status, and linked Purchase Orders or Transfer Orders, ensuring complete transparency and accountability across teams.

3. **Seamless Inventory & Procurement Integration**

Easily convert approved material requests into **Transfer Orders** (for internal stock movement) or **Purchase Orders** (for vendor procurement). Real-time integration keeps your inventory updated and eliminates duplicate entries.

4. **Improved Field Efficiency**

Enable technicians to raise material requests directly from the mobile app, whether on-site or in the field. This reduces communication gaps, accelerates approvals, and helps ensure that required materials reach the job location on time.

5. **Approval & Prioritization Control**

Configure approval workflows and set material request priorities (Low, Medium, High, Urgent) to ensure critical items are processed first. This adds governance and improves turnaround time for high-priority work.

6. **Enhanced Traceability & Audit Readiness**

Every request is automatically linked to the associated Job, Quote, or Purchase Order, creating a clear audit trail from request initiation to receipt, ensuring accuracy and accountability throughout the material lifecycle.

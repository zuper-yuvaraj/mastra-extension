# Subcontractors

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Vendors → + New → Subcontractor*
</Frame>

## Overview

Subcontractors are external companies or individuals you engage to carry out work on your behalf. Instead of tracking them separately, Zuper lets you add them directly inside the **Vendors** module, so their details sit alongside your other external partners in one place.

When you raise a purchase order, Zuper pulls the subcontractor's details automatically from the saved record, which saves you from re-entering the same information each time. This article covers how to add a new subcontractor and how to edit a saved record if details change later.

<Note>
  Fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required. You cannot save the record without completing them.
</Note>

***

## Add a subcontractor

1. Select the **Vendors** module from the left navigation menu.
2. Select **+ New** at the top right of the page. A dropdown appears with two options.
3. Select **Subcontractor**. The **New Subcontractor** page opens.

## Enter primary details

The primary details section captures the subcontractor's identity and contact information. Work through each field in order, then move on to transaction details.

1. Under **Primary Details**, enter the **Subcontractor Name**. This is the legal or trading name of the subcontractor.
2. Enter the **Subcontractor Contact Name**. This is the primary contact person at the subcontractor company.
3. Enter the **Subcontractor Display Name**. This is a short name shown in lists and documents.
4. Enter the **Subcontractor Email**. This is the main email address for the subcontractor.
5. Enter the **Subcontractor Work Number**. This is the primary phone number.
6. Enter the **Subcontractor Mobile Number** if one is available.

***

## Enter transaction details

Transaction details control how Zuper handles payment and tax calculations for this subcontractor on purchase orders.

1. Under **Transaction Details**, select a **Payment Term** from the list. This is the agreed payment timeline between your business and the subcontractor.
2. Enter the **Subcontractor Tax ID** if you have one. This is the subcontractor's tax registration number.
3. Select a **Default Tax Rate** from the list if applicable. This is the tax rate Zuper applies by default to transactions with this subcontractor.

## Add a billing address

The billing address is required before you can save the record. You will find this section in the right panel of the page.

1. In the right panel, select **Billing Address**.
2. Enter the **Street Address**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code**.
3. Select the **Country** from the list.

<Note>
  **Billing Address is required.** You cannot save the subcontractor record without adding one.
</Note>

## Add bank details

Bank details are optional at this stage. You can leave this section blank now and return to fill it in later.

1. In the right panel, select **Bank Details**.
2. Enter the **Name on Account**.
3. Enter the **Account Number**.
4. Enter the **Bank Name**.
5. Enter the **Branch Identifier**. This is the branch or routing code for the subcontractor's bank.

<Tip>
  If you do not have bank details on hand, you can skip this section and add it after saving. To update the record later, open it from the Vendors listing page, select **More Actions**, then select **Edit**, and expand the **Bank Details** section in the right panel.
</Tip>

## Attach files and save

1. Select **Add Attachment** to upload any relevant files, for example, a signed contract or insurance certificate.
2. Select **Save Subcontractor** at the top right of the page.

Zuper saves the record and adds it to the **Vendors** listing page. The record appears under the **Subcontractor** type label so you can distinguish it from vendor records at a glance.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a vendor and a subcontractor in Zuper?">
    Both record types live in the **Vendors** module, but they serve different purposes. A vendor is a supplier from whom you purchase parts or materials. A subcontractor is a company or individual you engage to perform work on your behalf.

    Zuper keeps them in the same module so you can manage all external partners from one place. The **Type** column on the listing page shows clearly which record is which.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change a vendor record to a subcontractor after saving?">
    No. The record type is set at creation and cannot be changed after saving. If you created a record with the wrong type, create a new record with the correct type and transfer the details across.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I leave bank details blank?">
    Zuper saves the subcontractor record without bank details. You can add or edit them at any time by opening the record, selecting **More Actions**, then **Edit**, and expanding the **Bank Details** section in the right panel.

    If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

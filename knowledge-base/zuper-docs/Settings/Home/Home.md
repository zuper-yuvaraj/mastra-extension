# Settings Home

The Zuper Settings Home Page is your central hub for managing your workspace settings. Here’s a breakdown of the key sections and features to help you navigate and make the most of this page.

## Navigation Sidebar (Left Panel)

The sidebar provides quick access to various settings and tools within Zuper:

* **Back to Workspace**: Return to your main workspace.
* **Search Bar**: Quickly find specific settings or tools.
* **Home**: The current page shows an overview of settings.
* **Modules**: Manage and configure modules for your workspace.
* **Data Administration**: Handle data-related settings and permissions.
* **Security**: Adjust security settings to protect your workspace.
* **Users & Teams**: Manage user roles, permissions, and team structures.
* **Miscellaneous**: Access additional settings and configurations.
* **Developer Hub**: Explore developer tools, APIs, and integrations.
* **App Store**: Browse and install apps to enhance your workspace.
* **Support**: Get help or contact the support team.
* **User Profile**: View your profile and sign out.

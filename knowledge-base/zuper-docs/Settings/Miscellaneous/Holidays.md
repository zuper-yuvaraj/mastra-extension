# Holidays

Holiday settings allow you to define non-working days for your team, ensuring that jobs are not scheduled on those days if desired.

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Settings -> Miscellaneous -> Holidays*
</Frame>

1. From the settings, select “**Miscellaneous**." Under the "**Holidays**" section, choose “**+ Create New Holiday."**

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-emWp4p2JslOnfEr/images/Ho1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-emWp4p2JslOnfEr&q=85&s=abf16aaf91836e8a573e7493d4328337" alt="Ho1 Pn" width="1887" height="862" data-path="images/Ho1.png" />

2. Enter the following details.

   **Holiday Name**: Enter the holiday name.

   **Holiday Date**: Choose the holiday date.

   **Allow Jobs to be assigned on this day**? Choose "**Yes**" to assign the job on this day. Choose "**No**" to disable the option.

   **Is this a holiday for all the users**? Choose "**Yes**" to assign the holiday for all users on this day. Choose "**No**" to disable the option, which will open an additional option.

   **Choose applicable team (Dependent field)**: Choose the team for which the day is a working day.

   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-emWp4p2JslOnfEr/images/Ho2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-emWp4p2JslOnfEr&q=85&s=a70cfc93ad28acd35886eaef5c5d7f92" alt="Ho2 Pn" width="1914" height="863" data-path="images/Ho2.png" />

   Click "**Create**" to set the holiday.
3. The holiday is successfully created.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-emWp4p2JslOnfEr/images/Ho3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-emWp4p2JslOnfEr&q=85&s=83837706f4a478c111c55d19993fa3ee" alt="Ho3 Pn" width="1904" height="867" data-path="images/Ho3.png" />

4. The team filter and date picker can be done. Click <Icon icon="ellipsis-vertical" />icon to edit or delete the holiday.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-emWp4p2JslOnfEr/images/Ho4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-emWp4p2JslOnfEr&q=85&s=2ad21a339a8a08b503f4615784bbc51e" alt="Ho4 Pn" width="1916" height="868" data-path="images/Ho4.png" />

You can effectively manage holiday settings in Zuper to ensure your team’s schedule aligns with non-working days.

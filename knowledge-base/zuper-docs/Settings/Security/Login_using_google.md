# Login using Google

Sign in to Zuper with your Google account for a quick, secure experience. This article shows admins how to enable Google sign-in and walks users through logging in on the web and the mobile app.

## How do you enable sign-in with Google for your organization?

An admin with permission can manage Security settings.

1. Log in to your Zuper account. Under Settings, select the “**Security**” module from the left navigation menu.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU/images/Gg5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU&q=85&s=21d47b93ff38d42fbdee42ac66e2de3e" alt="Gg5 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Gg5.png" />

2. In the Security module, click **Sign-in & Password Policy**. This section lets you manage your organization's sign-in methods and password policies.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU/images/Gg6.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU&q=85&s=f18eee9a4baa1f6ee524cf19a1305158" alt="Gg6 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Gg6.png" />

3. Scroll to the bottom and find Enable Sign in with Google? Choose Yes from the dropdown. Click "**Save**" from the top right.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU/images/Gg7.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU&q=85&s=0c4b05be4bdcac8334d085c742c2e23c" alt="Gg7 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Gg7.png" />

<Note>
  **Note**: To disable it later, set "**Enable Sign in with Google?**" to "**No**" and save. The button is removed; existing sessions remain active until they expire or the user signs out.
</Note>

## Google login using Zuper Web App

1. Open your web browser and go to [https://web.zuperpro.com/login](https://web.zuperpro.com/login). Enter the company name, and you will be redirected to the login page.

Click the **Continue** \*\*with Google \*\*button below the username & password fields.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw/images/Gg1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw&q=85&s=c4ae5a11a761386fa1cec0150e7ff5da" alt="Gg1 Pn" width="1905" height="852" data-path="images/Gg1.png" />

2. You will be redirected to the Google sign-in screen, where you can enter your Google credentials to proceed.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw/images/Gg2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw&q=85&s=cbf7503806fc94923b512272c5fb10ba" alt="Gg2 Pn" width="1916" height="860" data-path="images/Gg2.png" />

3. Enter your Google password (or approve a passkey/2‑Step Verification), then click Next.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw/images/Gg3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw&q=85&s=f4ba060a52f4509726f084307ffc5f28" alt="Gg3 Pn" width="1916" height="855" data-path="images/Gg3.png" />

4. Review the access request (name and email address) when prompted and click **Continue**.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU/images/Gg4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU&q=85&s=6b5ef5d3ea800b46d62dcae480fa7b69" alt="Gg4 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Gg4.png" />

5. You’ll be redirected back to Zuper and signed in.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU/images/Gg8.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU&q=85&s=f8c464afa5cc1089d6730fd5e535a6b5" alt="Gg8 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/Gg8.png" />

## Google Login using Zuper Mobile App

Use your Google account to log in on iOS or Android.

**Requirements**

Your Zuper Admin has enabled “**Sign in with Google**.” Your Zuper user email matches your Google account email. You need the latest Zuper mobile app and a stable internet connection.

### Steps (iOS and Android)

Open the Zuper app. If you belong to multiple companies, tap Switch company (top‑right) and choose the correct workspace.

* Tap Sign in with Google.
* When prompted, tap Continue to allow the app to use [Google.com](http://google.com) to sign in.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw/images/Gg10.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw&q=85&s=54912e1771a38f180d688de58ee6981f" data-path="images/Gg10.png" />

*  Choose your Google account.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU/images/Gg9.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=R5Ezx_JIkgmN37AU&q=85&s=312771b62e952b8435fd4f3db15aa5a9" data-path="images/Gg9.png" />

* If you don’t see it, tap "**Use another account**," enter your Google email and password, or use your passkey, and complete any 2‑Step Verification your Google account requires.
* Review the access screen and tap Continue.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw/images/Gg12.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw&q=85&s=4f462b67b1cbf320a551ccaf524153cf" data-path="images/Gg12.png" />

* Your login to Zuper is successful, and you land on your Dashboard.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw/images/Gg13.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=AF6w1eP_wbcIbofw&q=85&s=d13708013136673095a8c3b1392c39e3" data-path="images/Gg13.png" />

With “**Sign in with Google**” enabled, your team can access Zuper quickly and securely across the web and mobile—there is no separate password to remember.

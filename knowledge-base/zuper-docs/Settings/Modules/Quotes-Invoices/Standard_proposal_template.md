# Standard Proposal Template

The proposal templates are predefined templates to link the service packages. This template is a basic format for creating a quote proposal. You can customize it to include specific details related to your products or services.

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Settings -> Modules -> Quotes and Invoices -> Proposal Templates*
</Frame>

1. Select the “**Settings**” module from the left panel. Under the “**Modules**,” Choose the “**Quotes & Invoices**.” Select the “**Proposal Templates**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/soDTkUPUm1xdb89y/images/QI3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=soDTkUPUm1xdb89y&q=85&s=611f2fd2912552eca0c495f666c53bdb" alt="QI3 Pn" width="1906" height="867" data-path="images/QI3.png" />

2. Select the “**Proposal Templates**” and click “**+ New Template**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/soDTkUPUm1xdb89y/images/QI20.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=soDTkUPUm1xdb89y&q=85&s=f9c4217b3a29d5aedd6572bc58d5ce35" alt="QI20 Pn" width="1906" height="869" data-path="images/QI20.png" />

2. Enter the template name and description. Click “**Proceed**” to enter the template details.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/soDTkUPUm1xdb89y/images/QI21.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=soDTkUPUm1xdb89y&q=85&s=7683ff32070d3a6a26f73f4bbc91b053" alt="QI21 Pn" width="1887" height="866" data-path="images/QI21.png" />

3. Upload the image. Add the service packages. Click “**Save**” to create the proposal template.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/soDTkUPUm1xdb89y/images/QI22.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=soDTkUPUm1xdb89y&q=85&s=f873242b448cd4d1ac5ca1ec4c6725ce" alt="QI22 Pn" width="1919" height="871" data-path="images/QI22.png" />

\
4\. Add-ons in the Proposal Template

When Add-ons are configured and linked to service packages, they automatically appear within proposal templates as **optional Add-ons**.

**Where Add-ons Appear?**

* Add-ons are displayed within each **proposal option card**, under a dedicated **Add-ons** section.
* They are grouped separately from the base service charges to clearly indicate that they are optional.

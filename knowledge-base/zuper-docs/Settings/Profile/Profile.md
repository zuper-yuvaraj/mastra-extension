# Edit Profile

The Zuper platform makes it easy to manage your account settings, ensuring your profile is up-to-date and secure.

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Top Right Corner -> Profile Pic - > Profile*
</Frame>

## **Edit Your Profile Preferences & Languages**

Update your details to keep your Zuper account accurate.

1. Once logged in to your Zuper Account, click your profile picture at the top right corner of the screen and click the "**Profile**."

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-gCTzqboWMxs3nCY/images/Prf1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-gCTzqboWMxs3nCY&q=85&s=f738869a2a0e26bf820ad9cfd7a29c83" alt="Prf1 Pn" width="1908" height="877" data-path="images/Prf1.png" />

2. A dialog box appears. Click the **Edit Profile** button to modify your Profile information and preferences. Update the fields as needed. Edit your preferred language.
   <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-gCTzqboWMxs3nCY/images/Prf2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-gCTzqboWMxs3nCY&q=85&s=49ec9c7d19b412584e95ee9ce72ff8b2" alt="Prf2 Pn" width="1917" height="870" data-path="images/Prf2.png" />
3. Once the changes are done, click the **Update** button to save your updated profile details.

## Languages support

Zuper supports multiple languages to help users work in their preferred language. You can change your language at any time in your profile settings.

**Available Languages:**

* **English**
* **Italian**
* **Spanish**
* **French**
* **German**
* **Portuguese**
* **Portuguese (Brazilian)**
* **Dutch**
* **Russian**
* **Indonesian (Bahasa)**
* **French (Canada)**
* **Korean**
* **Slovak**

Zuper platform simplifies account management by providing an intuitive interface to update and secure your profile effortlessly. With easy access to settings, you can ensure your information remains current and protected, enhancing your overall experience on the platform.

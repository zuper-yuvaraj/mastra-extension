# Set up communications

> Keep your roofing team and customers connected with calls and texts built right into Zuper.

> Keep every conversation with your customers and crews in one place.

Roofing work moves constantly between the office, the field, and the customer's front door. Homeowners call about leaks, leads follow up on quotes, and crews check in from the job site throughout the day.

When these conversations happen across personal phones and separate apps, details get lost, and follow-ups slip through the cracks. Zuper Connect keeps every call and text inside Zuper, so your team stays in touch without leaving the platform.

Your business gets a dedicated phone number and a shared inbox for calls and texts. Routing rules then send each conversation to the right person, whether that is a sales rep or a dispatcher. Every call and text stays logged against the customer record, giving your team full context to continue the conversation.

Ready to get started? See the [Zuper Connect overview](https://docs.zuper.co/Zuper_Connect/Overview) to set up your number, permissions, and call routing.

# E-Check Deposit on Mobile

The E-Check feature in Zuper Pay enables you to collect and deposit customer check payments digitally, eliminating the need for physical bank visits. By capturing check images with your device, you can process deposits quickly and securely while tracking payments within the Zuper platform.

<Check>
  **Perquisite**:  Account configuration is required to use this feature. Please contact our Support team for details.
</Check>

<Note>
  **Note:** The E-Check feature is available only on the Zuper mobile application.
</Note>

## **How to Deposit an E-Check**

Follow these steps to deposit a check:

1. Navigate to the relevant Invoice or quote and click **Collect Payment**.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/XV5-Yi6r56cUyF5X/Zuper-pay/images/check-1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XV5-Yi6r56cUyF5X&q=85&s=196a2ebde26be048c2846076481b09de" alt="Check 1 Pn" className="mx-auto" style={{ width:"50%" }} width="1419" height="2796" data-path="Zuper-pay/images/check-1.png" />
</Frame>

2. Choose **Deposit Check**.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/XV5-Yi6r56cUyF5X/Zuper-pay/images/check-2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XV5-Yi6r56cUyF5X&q=85&s=b8b2d33fa58b9091a45434e28cda6a8b" alt="Check 2 Pn" className="mx-auto" style={{ width:"51%" }} width="1419" height="2796" data-path="Zuper-pay/images/check-2.png" />
</Frame>

3. Your device’s camera will open automatically. Capture:
   * A clear landscape photo of the **front** of the check. <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/2kF6ttZhRDv6pqMu/Zuper-pay/images/frontimage.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=2kF6ttZhRDv6pqMu&q=85&s=2e32df8f43431870e392b93f73e036b6" alt="Frontimage Pn" width="602" height="297" data-path="Zuper-pay/images/frontimage.png" />

* A clear landscape photo of the **front** of the check. <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/XV5-Yi6r56cUyF5X/Zuper-pay/images/backimage.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=XV5-Yi6r56cUyF5X&q=85&s=b4f1496b87eef159af834de4a241656b" alt="Backimage Pn" width="602" height="314" data-path="Zuper-pay/images/backimage.png" />

<Note>
  **Note**: A charge of \$0.75 will apply if the images are blurry or unclear.
</Note>

4. The due amount fills in automatically. You can edit the amount if needed.
   <Note>
     **Note**: If you’re depositing against a quote, you *cannot* edit the amount.
   </Note>
5. Enter the **Check Number**.
6. Click **Proceed**. Note that a \$1.00 processing fee applies per transaction. If the check bounces, a \$20 fee will be charged (similar to traditional banks).
7. After you proceed, a confirmation message will appear stating, **“Check deposited successfully.”**

### **How to View Payment Details**

After a successful deposit, you can view payment details under:

* **Payment Transactions** → **Associated** tab.

This section provides a comprehensive record of all payments collected for the related transaction.

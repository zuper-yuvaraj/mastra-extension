# Dashboard 101: Customization

The power of the **Zuper Dashboard** lies in its flexibility and real-time insights. By customizing your dashboard, you can focus on the metrics that drive your business forward—whether that’s tracking job progress, monitoring user performance, managing financials, or staying on top of customer requests.

With Zuper, you’re not stuck with a one-size-fits-all dashboard. Instead, you can tailor your view to display exactly what matters most to you and adjust it as your needs evolve. Plus, the real-time updates ensure you’re always making decisions based on the latest information, enhancing your ability to respond swiftly to changing circumstances.

In this guide, we’ll walk you through how to make your Zuper Dashboard truly your own. Let’s get started!

2. You can view the default dashboard with various key metrics from each module.<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d/Zuper_Dashboard/DashDownload-5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d&q=85&s=4486eb5195f0a58d8c9bb19d2769b57c" alt="" width="1908" height="939" data-path="Zuper_Dashboard/DashDownload-5.png" />
3. Click the "**Customize**" button at the top right of the page.

The customization panel opens, letting you tailor your dashboard to your exact needs.  To explore each module and its measurable key metrics, click here.

### Adding widgets to your dashboard

Widgets are the building blocks of your Zuper Dashboard, allowing you to display specific metrics like job statistics, time off requests, or invoices.

1. Click "**+ Add Widget**" in the customization panel.<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d/Zuper_Dashboard/Dash-17.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d&q=85&s=065a9e8e7d093e1d65fbb2b7400f23fc" alt="" width="1920" height="827" data-path="Zuper_Dashboard/Dash-17.png" />
2. Choose from a variety of widget options (e.g., Job Statistics, Timeoff request, or invoice).
3. Click "**Add**" to place the widget on your dashboard.<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d/Zuper_Dashboard/Dash-18.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d&q=85&s=f90bb826284d795f13e8e59e225b27b2" alt="" width="1920" height="827" data-path="Zuper_Dashboard/Dash-18.png" />
4. Drag the widget to your desired spot and resize it for optimal viewing.
5. Click "**Save**" to apply the changes.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d/Zuper_Dashboard/DashDownload-3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d&q=85&s=e346000fe3d8161efb1d1affa5af7e07" alt="" width="1929" height="942" data-path="Zuper_Dashboard/DashDownload-3.png" />

The new widget will be successfully added to the dashboard.

You can also streamline your dashboard by removing or updating the widgets as you prefer. To do so, click the **edit** or **remove** icon on the widget.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d/Zuper_Dashboard/DashDownload-2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d&q=85&s=9d1c0368a581bec34fec29cf1179828c" alt="" width="1899" height="938" data-path="Zuper_Dashboard/DashDownload-2.png" />

## Customizing Dashboard Metrics: *Time Frames*, *Categories*, and *Teams*

Your dashboard metrics can also be adjusted by selecting different time frames, job categories, and teams. Using the actions menu at the top right, you can filter data by **Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Custom Ranges**, refine insights based on job categories, and display team-specific information.
Applying these filters ensures that you only see the most relevant data for effective decision-making.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d/Zuper_Dashboard/Dash-13.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d&q=85&s=c4995bda99865477f93304e4c6c9d46b" alt="" width="1920" height="827" data-path="Zuper_Dashboard/Dash-13.png" />

## Downloading Graphical Representations of Metrics

You can download graphical representations of metrics by clicking the Download icon at the top-right corner of the widget **Download** icon on the widget’s top-right corner. The widget will be saved as an image file, which can be used for presentations, reports, or internal discussions.<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d/Zuper_Dashboard/Dashdown.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=-k3Bjeas_7cp9V3d&q=85&s=3cd424089542289b0e0e92d66f93e1bd" alt="" width="1905" height="938" data-path="Zuper_Dashboard/Dashdown.png" />

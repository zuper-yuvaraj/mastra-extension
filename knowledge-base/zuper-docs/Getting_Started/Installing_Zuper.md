# Installing Zuper

Give your field teams the information and technology they need on-site with our mobile app. Enable your field teams to manage jobs, schedules, and customer information within the app while staying connected with the back office.

## iOS

1. Open the **App Store** on your device. Alternatively, click this [link](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/zuper-field-service-your-way/id1633081340). Search for “**Zuper- Field Service Your Way**” and select it from the results. Tap the ⬇️ icon to install the app.
2. Once installed, open the app and enter your “**Company Name**” to proceed. Enter your “**Email ID**” and “**Password**,” then click the “**Login**” button.

## Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your device. Alternatively, click this [link](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.zuper.android). Search for “**Zuper- Field Service App**” and select it from the results. Tap the "**Install**" button to install the apps.
2. Once installed, open the app and enter your “**Company Name**” to proceed. Enter your “**Email ID**” and “**Password**,” then click the “**Login**” button.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC/images/Android4.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC&q=85&s=c3382bf5f39ca7c7a3040cdfb34dc61c" data-path="images/Android4.jpg" />

<Note>
  Note: The **Zuper Field Service App** is the current, latest mobile application. The **Zuper Pro Mobile App** is the older version and is no longer the latest.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my account locked, and how do I log in again?">
    If you enter an incorrect password 3 times, Zuper blacklists your network IP for 30 minutes due to security policy. You can sign in again after 30 mins or try with a different network. <br />If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Web

1. Open your web browser and go to [https://web.zuperpro.com/login](https://web.zuperpro.com/login)
2. Enter your "**Company Name**," and on the next page, enter your "**Email ID**" and "**Password,**" then click the "**Login**" button to enter Zuper.

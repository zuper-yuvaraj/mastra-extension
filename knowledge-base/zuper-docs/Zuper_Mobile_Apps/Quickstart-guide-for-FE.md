# Quickstart Guide

> This guide provides field technicians with essential steps to navigate and use the Zuper Mobile App efficiently.

# Overview

The Zuper Mobile App is designed to help Field Executives efficiently manage their daily field operations. With a streamlined interface and quick access to essential job information, the app enables you to:

* View and manage assigned jobs
* Update job status in real-time
* Complete service tasks and checklists
* Communicate with customers and your internal team
* Add notes, upload photos/videos, and log activities

The mobile app ensures that every FE stays informed, organized, and productive throughout the workday.

# Downloading and Installing the Zuper Field Service App

1. Open the **Google Play Store** (Android) or **App Store** (iOS) on your mobile device.
2. In the search bar, type **“Zuper- Field Service App.”**
3. From the search results, select the Zuper app.
4. Tap **Install** (Android) or **Get** (iOS).

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/SRi64imnmqNfn83u/images/Install.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=SRi64imnmqNfn83u&q=85&s=931d2b7cb7748eb7f4cafc26cd12fe95" alt="Install Pn" width="2000" height="2000" data-path="images/Install.png" />

5. Once installed, tap **Open** or click the **Zuper** app icon on your home screen to begin using the app.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/RLbtgPWHv7gTUVUr/images/Open.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=RLbtgPWHv7gTUVUr&q=85&s=949dd91b520de00bafbd022e390b9d08" alt="Open Pn" width="2000" height="2000" data-path="images/Open.png" />

For more information on device requirements for a smooth performance, check out this [article](https://docs.zuper.co/Zuper_Mobile_Apps/Device_Requirements).

# Logging In to the Mobile App

1. Open the **Zuper Field Service App** on your mobile device. Enter the **Company Name** and tap **Proceed** to continue.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/rlU9quLKn18OxWC2/images/company.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=rlU9quLKn18OxWC2&q=85&s=3dd315468a5eb50f7c14e91923e5da99" alt="Company Pn" width="2000" height="2000" data-path="images/company.png" />

3. Enter your **registered email** and **password** provided by your administrator.
4. Tap **Login**.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/nSNfgtyjaTXPb8DJ/images/login.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=nSNfgtyjaTXPb8DJ&q=85&s=6f3eb0e1a556c02118ffae99289ab4de" alt="Login Pn" width="2000" height="2000" data-path="images/login.png" />

3. After successful login, you will be redirected to the **Dashboard**.

**Note:** You can also log in using **Sign in with Google** if your organization has enabled Google authentication. This allows you to access the app quickly without manually entering your password.

<Accordion title="Forgot Password?">
  If you have forgotten your password:

  1. On the **login screen**, tap **Forgot Password?**
  2. Enter your **Company Name** and registered **email address**.
  3. Tap **Send**. You will receive an email with **password reset instructions**.
  4. Follow the instructions in the email to create a **new password**.
  5. Return to the app and log in using your **new password**.

  <Note>
    **Note:**

    * If you don’t receive the email, check your **spam/junk folder**.
    * For further assistance, contact your administrator.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

### **Offline upload**

If your device is offline when you upload a file, the upload is saved locally on your device and queued for sync. However, if the app closes or your device restarts before a connection is restored, that upload might not persist. Always confirm a successful sync using the **Activity (Jobs → Job Details Page → Context Menu → Activity)** log before leaving a job site.

<Accordion title="Offline ">
  #### How uploads work

  When you upload a document or image on the mobile app:

  * The file is queued on your device immediately.
  * The app syncs the file to Zuper as soon as an internet connection is available.
  * A sync indicator appears on the upload thumbnail until the file has successfully uploaded.
  * Once synced, the file appears in the job's **Notes** tab or **Gallery** tab and is visible to your team.

  > **Important:** Uploads queued while offline might not persist if the app is force-closed or your device restarts before a connection is restored. Upload files while connected whenever possible.

  #### **Upload a document or image to a job note**

  1. Tap the job you want to update from your assigned jobs list.
  2. Tap the **Add note** icon from the quick actions bar.
  3. From the **Document** list, select the file type that matches what you want to upload — for example, **Document** for a PDF or **Photo** for an image.
  4. Select the file from your device or capture a new photo.
  5. Type a description in the note field if needed.
  6. Tap **Post Note** to save and queue the upload.

  > **Tip:** You can attach up to five files per note. Each file must be 20 MB or smaller. Supported file types include images, videos, audio, PDF, Docs, Excel, archives, and other standard file formats.

  #### Check if your upload synced successfully

  After uploading a file, confirm it synced before you leave the job site.

  1. Tap the job the file was uploaded to.
  2. Swipe to the **Activity** tab on the job details page.
  3. Look for an upload event entry that matches the file name and timestamp.
  4. If the entry appears, the file has synced successfully.

  > **Note:** If you uploaded the file while offline, the **Activity** log entry appears only after your device reconnects and the sync completes. Wait for a connection and then check the log again.

  #### What to do if you are offline when you upload

  If you know you are in an area with no or poor connectivity:

  1. Complete the upload steps as normal — the file is queued on your device.
  2. Keep the app open and in the foreground until you regain a connection.
  3. Once your device shows a connection, wait for the sync indicator on the upload thumbnail to disappear.
  4. Go to the **Activity** tab on the job to confirm the upload event appears.

  > **Tip:** Do not force-close the app or restart your device while files are waiting to sync. Doing so might cause queued uploads to be lost.
</Accordion>

# Using the App in Offline Mode

The Zuper Mobile App supports an **Offline Mode**- allowing you to keep working even in areas with limited or no internet connectivity.

**Enabling Offline Mode**

* The offline feature must first be enabled for your organization via the Zuper Admin portal.
* Once enabled, you can turn on offline mode from the **Settings**>>**Configuration**>>**Offline Mode**.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/CcrTcVZxJCNlA38N/images/offline.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=CcrTcVZxJCNlA38N&q=85&s=bd89353d77c31dcc26cf829563c2ba52" alt="Offline Pn" width="2000" height="2000" data-path="images/offline.png" />

* After switching on offline mode and when the necessary data is downloaded, the app status will show as **“Ready for offline usage.”**

**What Data is Available Offline**

When offline is enabled and primed, the app stores local copies of all relevant records tied to the user’s assigned jobs (for a window around ±3 days). This includes: job details, related customers/organizations, assets, properties, contracts, parts & services, and routes for assigned work.

However, note that some modules are **not** primed offline — for example, quotes, invoices, and other unrelated records.

**What You Can Do Offline**

While offline, you can

* View assigned jobs and job details (with keyword search for those jobs)
* Update job status and checklists (including uploading pictures as part of the checklist)
* Add job notes and attachments (photos/videos/documents) tied to a job.
* View and manage service tasks assigned to jobs — including viewing associated assets, filling inspection forms, viewing submitted forms, and updating task status. If a service task was unassigned, updating it offline will auto-assign it to the technician who updated it.
* View cached data about customers, assets, organizations, properties, parts & services relevant to their assigned jobs.

Once your device reconnects to the internet, all offline changes (status updates, notes, checklist completions, service-task updates) are queued and automatically synced with the backend. The app dashboard shows the upload status to help you track pending syncs.

**⚠️ Limitations & Considerations**

* Only records linked to jobs assigned to the user are primed offline. Even if a user is an Admin or Team Lead, jobs not assigned to them are **not** available offline.
* Only up to 2 levels of associated records are primed. For example, jobs → customers → organizations/assets; but deeper linked records or unrelated modules are not available offline.
* Some features are **not** supported offline: e.g., accepting or rejecting new job assignments must be done online.
* Modules such as chat, notifications, and real-time updates do not work offline.

### Understanding in-app network indicators

When the Zuper app detects connectivity issues, it surfaces indicators rather than crashing or showing blank screens. Here is what each one means:

| Indicator | What it means | What to do |
| :- | :- | :- |
| **"Poor connection. Showing saved jobs only."** | The app is serving cached data. Live sync is unavailable. | Check your network connection. If you are in an area with low signal, this is expected. See Using Zuper Offline for how cached data works. |
| **"Last refreshed \[time]"** | A record has not synced recently due to poor signal. | Move to a stronger network and pull to refresh the record. |
| **"Showing limited details"** | The app could not load the complete record due to a weak connection. | Retry on a stronger network. |
| **"Unable to load latest updates"** | The record is outdated and the app cannot refresh it. | Check your connection and try again. If offline mode is enabled, the record may be serving cached data from a previous session. |

If you are not seeing any of these indicators but are still getting errors, continue to the steps below.

***

Troubleshooting network errors on the Zuper mobile app

### Step 1: Basic checks

Run these first regardless of the device or network:

1. Open a browser on the affected device and visit any website. If it does not load, the network itself is the issue — not Zuper.
2. Force-close the Zuper app completely and reopen it.
3. Toggle Wi-Fi off, wait ten seconds, and toggle it back on.
4. If you are in an area with a weak signal, move closer to the router or switch to cellular and retry.
5. Log out of the Zuper app and log back in. This restarts the data priming process.

\<Note> When you log in, the Zuper app automatically downloads job data for your assigned jobs (data priming). If a network error occurs during priming, an error message appears and priming stops. Logging out and back in on a stable connection restarts this process. \</Note>

If the issue only affects one device while another device on the same network works fine, continue to Step 2.

***

### Step 2: Device-specific diagnosis

When one device shows errors and another on the same Wi-Fi network does not, the root cause is almost always a device-level setting. Work through the following:

#### App and OS version

| Check | Steps |
| :- | :- |
| **App version mismatch** | Open the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android) on the affected device. Check whether a Zuper update is available and install it. Confirm both devices are on the same version. |
| **Outdated OS** | Go to Settings → General → Software Update. Install any pending updates. |

#### Network and connectivity settings

### Network and connectivity settings

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Wi-Fi Assist">
    **What it does:** When Wi-Fi is weak, the device automatically switches to cellular data. This can cause Zuper to drop and re-establish its connection intermittently, producing network errors even when Wi-Fi appears connected.

    **Note:** Wi-Fi Assist is only available on cellular-capable iPads. If the affected iPad is a Wi-Fi-only model, skip this check.

    1. Open **Settings**.
    2. Tap **Cellular** (or **Mobile Data**, depending on your region).
    3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen.
    4. Toggle **Wi-Fi Assist** off.
    5. Reopen Zuper and retest.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Background App Refresh">
    **What it does:** Controls whether Zuper can sync data in the background when you switch to another app. If disabled, the app stops updating until you reopen it, which can cause stale or missing data on return.

    1. Open **Settings**.
    2. Tap **General**.
    3. Tap **Background App Refresh**.
    4. At the top, confirm the master toggle is set to **Wi-Fi** or **Wi-Fi & Cellular Data** — not **Off**.
    5. Scroll down to **Zuper** and confirm its individual toggle is on.
    6. Reopen Zuper and retest.

    <Note>
      Background App Refresh is automatically disabled when Low Power Mode is active. If the affected device is in Low Power Mode, disable it under **Settings → Battery** and retest.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="VPN active on one device">
    **What it does:** A VPN running on the affected device but not the other may route traffic differently or block certain Zuper endpoints, causing intermittent errors that appear network-related.

    1. Open **Settings**.
    2. Tap **General**.
    3. Tap **VPN & Device Management**.
    4. Under **VPN**, check whether a VPN configuration is listed and connected.
    5. If a VPN is active, disconnect it temporarily and retest Zuper.
    6. If the error disappears, contact your IT administrator — the VPN profile may need a Zuper-specific exception.

    <Note>
      On MDM-managed iPads, a VPN profile may be enforced by your organization and cannot be disabled from the device. In that case, escalate to your IT administrator with the Zuper error details and the screen recording.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

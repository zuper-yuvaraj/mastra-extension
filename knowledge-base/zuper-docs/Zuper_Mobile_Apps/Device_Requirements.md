# Device Requirements

The Zuper app has specific minimum requirements for Android and iOS devices to ensure smooth performance. Meeting these requirements will enable you to utilize all the app's features and functionalities effectively.

## Android device requirements

<Note>
  Minimum Android version: Android 8.0 (Oreo)
</Note>

To check the software version on your Android device, follow these steps:

1. Open "**Settings**" on your mobile and tap "**About device/phone.**"

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC/images/And1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC&q=85&s=2f0a905933c3c2123ca6d0f47b405be5" data-path="images/And1.png" />

2. Scroll down to view the "**Android Version**." (Ensure it is at least version Android 8.0 Oreo for the best experience).

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC/images/And3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=foY4ItPnEcbwC0iC&q=85&s=fc58b5910a61d055793076a915536c88" data-path="images/And3.png" />

For a seamless Zuper app experience, keep your device updated to the latest Android version.

## iOS device requirements

<Note>
  Minimum iOS version: 17.0 or later. For the best experience, we recommend iOS 26 or later
</Note>

To check the software version on your iOS device, follow these steps:

1. Open "**Settings**" on your iOS mobile and tap "**General**"

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/WWRryQiWANaj5aW3/images/ios7-portrait.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=WWRryQiWANaj5aW3&q=85&s=7813a412748a48e547334f3cc3092300" data-path="images/ios7-portrait.png" />

2. Select "**General.**"

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/WWRryQiWANaj5aW3/images/ioss1-portrait.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=WWRryQiWANaj5aW3&q=85&s=da316413966bcf6d095bf4d319f76e07" data-path="images/ioss1-portrait.png" />

3. Select "**About**" to view the iOS version.

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/WWRryQiWANaj5aW3/images/ioss2-portrait.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=WWRryQiWANaj5aW3&q=85&s=00b821da9e8ef41beb8e9e7c54281d04" data-path="images/ioss2-portrait.png" />

4. Make sure your device runs on 15.0 or later (For an optimal experience, we recommend iOS 16.2 or later).

<img width="250" height="200" src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/WWRryQiWANaj5aW3/images/ioss3-portrait.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=WWRryQiWANaj5aW3&q=85&s=ef1bf2699fb87d2337ae7a77964d04d8" data-path="images/ioss3-portrait.png" />

By meeting these requirements, you ensure smooth and efficient use of Zuper app on your mobile device.

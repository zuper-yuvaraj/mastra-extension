# Managing your reports 

In Zuper, the **Reports home page** serves as a centralized workspace where you can view, manage, and organize all available reports. The page provides access to reports you have created or shared with you and allows you to perform several actions, such as creating, scheduling, editing, and deleting reports. 

<Check>
  **Report Access Permissions by Role** \
  Your user role determines access to reports in Zuper: 

  * **Admins**: Can create, edit, and delete reports, as well as share and schedule them. 
  * **Team Leaders (TLs)**: Can view reports that have been shared with them, but cannot create, edit, or delete reports. 
  * **Users (FEs)**: Cannot access reports in Zuper. 
</Check>

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to explore, manage, and interact with reports within Zuper. 

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Reports -> Reports (beta)*
</Frame>

## Accessing the Reports home page

1. Click the **Reports** module from the left navigation menu and select "**Reports**" (beta).  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/UzQXhsiJl1KJlGVR/Reports/images/reports6.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=UzQXhsiJl1KJlGVR&q=85&s=eb471f53f0a79723c0bff4b7ce4c321c" alt="Reports6 Pn" width="1904" height="861" data-path="Reports/images/reports6.png" />
2. The **Reports home page** will open, displaying a comprehensive list of all reports available to you. 

The home page is your dashboard for managing existing reports, creating new reports, and scheduling reports for automated delivery.

## Actions and features on the Reports list

Next to each report in the Reports List, there are several actions you can take. These functions allow you to manage your reports efficiently: 

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/UzQXhsiJl1KJlGVR/Reports/images/reports21.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=UzQXhsiJl1KJlGVR&q=85&s=f41e1f46424a8651d87ce054f5952ff1" alt="Reports21 Pn" width="1896" height="694" data-path="Reports/images/reports21.png" />

* **Share Report:** Share a report with other users or teams and define their access permissions, such as view-only or edit permissions. 
  <Note>
    ***Note:*** The Share option appears when you hover over the report you want to share.\_ 
  </Note>
* **Schedule Report:** Set up an automated schedule for reports to be generated and sent to specific recipients at defined intervals. 
  <Note>
    ***Note:*** *The Schedule option appears when you hover over the respective report.* 
  </Note>
* **Move Report:** Organize the report by moving it into an existing folder or creating a new folder for better categorization. 
* **Edit Report:** Modify the parameters, filters, or layout of the report. 
* **Clone Report:** Make a copy of the existing report. The cloned report will retain the original settings but can be edited independently. 

<Note>
  **Note:** There is a limit on the number of reports you can clone. If you select **Clone Report** and a **Cloning** pop-up appears and then disappears without creating a new report, you have reached your cloning limit. Contact [Zuper Support](mailto:support@zuper.co) to request a limit increase.
</Note>

* **Delete Report:** Permanently remove the report from the system. Deletion is irreversible, so ensure that you no longer need the report before deleting it. 

These actions give you control over your reports, allowing for easy sharing, scheduling, and organizing based on your needs.

## Troubleshooting

### Greyed-out fields in the field selector

**Symptom** A field in the **Available Fields** panel appears greyed out and you cannot select it.

**Cause** The field is already added to your report. Zuper greys out fields that are currently in use to prevent duplicates.

**Fix** No action is needed. If you want to use the field in a different position, remove it from your report first, then add it again.

1. Locate the field in your report's column list.
2. Select the remove icon next to the field.
3. Add the field again in the position you want.

<Tip>
  Greyed-out fields are not unavailable — they are already part of your report. Look for them in the columns you have already added.
</Tip>

***

### Expense Created At filter

**Symptom** You are building a report on expenses and want to filter by the date an expense was created, but you are unsure which filter to use.

**Cause** The **Expense Created At** filter is available in the Expenses module within Report Builder. It filters expense records by the date and time they were created in Zuper.

**Fix**

1. Select **Reports** from the left navigation menu and select **Reports**.
2. Open your expense report or create a new one using the Expenses module.
3. Go to the **Filters** panel.
4. Select **Expense Created At** from the filter list.
5. Enter the date range you want to report on.
6. Select **Apply**.

***

### Expense Category filter returns no results or an error

**Symptom** You apply the **Expense Category** filter in your report and receive no results or an unexpected error message.

**Cause** This is a known issue. The **Expense Category** filter does not return results as expected in some configurations.

**Fix** There is no workaround available at this time. Avoid using the **Expense Category** filter until Zuper confirms the fix is deployed.

<Warning>
  Do not rely on the **Expense Category** filter for critical reporting until this issue is resolved. Use other available filters such as, **Expense Created At** to narrow your results in the meantime.
</Warning>

If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).

***

## Report Builder is not visible in my account

**Symptom** You do not see **Reports** (beta) in your left navigation menu, or the option is unavailable.

**Cause** Report Builder is a paid add-on. It is not included in the standard Zuper plan.

**Fix** Contact your Zuper account manager to confirm whether Report Builder is included in your plan, or to request access.

If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).

## FAQs

1. **Is there a limit on how many reports I can clone?** \
   Yes. Zuper sets a default cloning limit for reports. If you need a higher limit, contact [Zuper Support](mailto:support@zuper.co) so the team can review your account and discuss your options.
2. **What should I do if the Cloning pop-up keeps disappearing?** \
      1. Confirm that you have not reached the cloning limit for your account. \
      2. Refresh the page and try again. \
      3. If the issue continues, contact [Zuper Support.](mailto:support@zuper.co)
3. **Why are some fields greyed out in the Available Fields panel?** \
   Greyed-out fields are already added to your report. Zuper prevents you from adding the same field twice. Remove the field from your current column list first, then add it in the position you want.
4. **Is Report Builder included in my Zuper plan?** \
   Report Builder is a paid add-on and is not included in all plans. Contact your Zuper account manager for pricing details.
5. **Can I still use Legacy Reports if I have Report Builder?** \
   Yes. Legacy Reports remain available to all users under the **Legacy Reports** section in the left navigation menu, regardless of whether you have Report Builder enabled.
   If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).

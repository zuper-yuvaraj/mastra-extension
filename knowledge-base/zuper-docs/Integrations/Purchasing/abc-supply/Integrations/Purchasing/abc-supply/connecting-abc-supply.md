# Connecting ABC Supply

<Info>
  This feature is currently in beta. To enable it for your account, contact your Account Manager or reach out to [support@zuper.co](mailto:support@zuper.co).
</Info>

<Frame>
  **Navigation**: *Settings → Parts and Services → Supplier Integrations → ABC Supply*
</Frame>

## Overview

The ABC Supply integration connects Zuper to your myABCsupply account, so your team can order roofing materials without switching between systems. Once connected, Zuper pulls live, branch-specific pricing into every estimate and material order you create.

When an order ships, delivery updates appear directly on the material order in Zuper, giving everyone on your team a single place to track materials from quote to delivery.

To get started, you install the app and sign in with your myABCsupply credentials. ABC Supply uses a secure OAuth sign-in to verify your account, and the sections below walk you through each step in order.

## Before you begin

Have the following ready before you install the integration, since gathering this in advance prevents interruptions during setup.

* Your myABCsupply username and password. If you do not have a myABCsupply account, contact your ABC Supply branch representative to set one up before you continue.

## Sign in to your ABC Supply account

Zuper uses OAuth to connect to your ABC Supply account, so you sign in directly through ABC Supply's secure portal.

1. Enter your myABCsupply username in the **Username** field.
2. Enter your password in the **Password** field.

3) Select **Sign in**. Zuper verifies your credentials with ABC Supply, and when the connection succeeds, your account is linked and Zuper loads your branch information.

<Note>
  Select **Keep me signed in** on the sign-in screen if you want to stay connected without signing in again each session.
</Note>

<Note>
  If the sign-in screen does not open, or it returns an error, confirm that your myABCsupply account is active and that your credentials are correct. If the issue continues, contact [Support](mailto:support@zuper.co).
</Note>

## Choose your branches

After you sign in, Zuper loads the ABC Supply branches linked to your account, and you select the branches you order from and assign a **Ship-To Account** to each one.

1. Review the list of ABC Supply branches linked to your account.
2. Select the checkbox next to each branch you want to use in Zuper.
3. Open the **Ship-To Account** dropdown for each selected branch, and select the account to use for orders at that branch.
4. Select the **star icon** next to a branch to set it as your preferred branch. Zuper uses the preferred branch for daily pricing updates across your catalog.
5. Select **Save** to confirm your branch selections.

<Note>
  You must assign a **Ship-To Account** to every branch you select, and you can update your branch selections and preferred branch at any time from **Supplier Settings**.
</Note>

## Import your parts catalog

Zuper imports your product catalog from the order templates linked to your ABC Supply account, so importing from templates keeps your **Parts and Services** catalog focused on what you actually use.

1. Review the list of templates available in your ABC Supply account on the **Select Template** step. Each row shows the **Template ID**, **Template Name**, and number of products.
2. Select the checkbox next to each template you want to import.
3. Select **Import from ABC Supply** to begin the import. Zuper imports the selected templates in the background, and each product becomes a part in your **Parts and Services** catalog. Pricing updates automatically each day at 6:30 AM UTC, or midnight CST.

<Note>
  Each template can only be imported once, and if you add new products to a template in ABC Supply after importing, those additions do not sync automatically. You can select the **Import Templates** tab in **Supplier Settings** at any time to bring in new templates.
</Note>

<Note>
  If no templates appear, your ABC Supply account does not have any order templates set up yet. Create templates of your frequently ordered products in your ABC Supply account, and then return to Zuper to complete the import.
</Note>

## Manage your branches

After setup, you can view and manage all connected ABC Supply branches from the **Branches** listing page. To get there, go to **Settings**, select **Parts and Services Settings**, select **Supplier Integrations**, and select **ABC Supply**.

<iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/1217577378?title=0&byline=0&portrait=0" title="Supplier Branch Selection ABC Supply" className="w-full aspect-video" frameBorder="0" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />

The **Branches** listing page shows each branch name, address, assigned **Ship-To Account**, and status.

### Add a branch

1. Go to **Settings**, select **Parts and Services Settings**, select **Supplier Integrations**, and select **ABC Supply**.
2. Select **+ Add Branch** at the top right.
3. Select the new branch from the list, and assign a **Ship-To Account** to it.
4. Select **Save**.

### Edit a branch

1. Hover over the branch you want to update on the **Branches** listing page.
2. Select the **pencil icon** that appears on the right.
3. Update the **Ship-To Account** details.
4. Select **Save**.

### Set a preferred branch

1. Locate the branch you want to set as your preferred branch on the **Branches** listing page.
2. Select the **star icon** next to it. A filled star indicates the preferred branch.

<Note>
  Zuper uses the preferred branch for daily catalog pricing updates, and only one branch can be set as preferred at a time. Selecting a new preferred branch removes the designation from the previous one.
</Note>

### Deactivate a branch

1. Hover over the branch you want to deactivate on the **Branches** listing page.
2. Select the **deactivate icon** that appears on the right.
3. Select **Deactivate** in the confirmation dialog to confirm.

<Note>
  Deactivated branches remain in your account and continue to receive pricing updates, but they are not available for selection when you create estimates or material orders.
</Note>

## Disconnect the ABC Supply integration

If you need to remove the ABC Supply integration from your Zuper account, you can uninstall it from the **App Store**.

1. Select your **profile icon**, select **App Store**, select **Purchasing**, and select **ABC Supply**.
2. Select **Uninstall App** on the left panel.
3. Select **Uninstall App** in the confirmation dialog to confirm.

<Warning>
  Uninstalling removes your account connection and branch configuration. Your existing parts and supplier records in Zuper are not deleted, but they might no longer receive ABC Supply pricing updates.
</Warning>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What credentials do I need to connect ABC Supply?">
    You need your myABCsupply username and password, and Zuper uses a secure OAuth sign-in. If you do not have a myABCsupply account, contact your ABC Supply branch representative to set one up before you continue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is OAuth, and is my password stored in Zuper?">
    OAuth is a secure, industry-standard method that lets two applications share access without sharing your password, so when you sign in through the ABC Supply portal, your credentials go directly to ABC Supply. Zuper receives only a secure token to identify your account, and your password is never stored in Zuper.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect more than one ABC Supply branch?">
    Yes, you can connect as many branches as your ABC Supply account supports, and each branch requires a **Ship-To Account** assignment before you can use it for orders.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the preferred branch do?">
    Zuper uses the preferred branch as a fallback when no branch is selected during pricing, and the preferred branch is also pre-filled in quotes and proposals.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to a deactivated branch?">
    A deactivated branch remains in your account and continues to receive daily pricing updates, but it is not available for selection when you create estimates or material orders. You can reactivate it at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will uninstalling the app delete my parts catalog?">
    No, your existing parts and supplier records in Zuper are not deleted when you uninstall the app, though those records no longer receive ABC Supply pricing updates.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

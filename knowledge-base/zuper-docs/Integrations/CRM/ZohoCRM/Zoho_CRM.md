# Zoho CRM

With Zuper’s first-class integration with Zoho CRM, Zuper seamlessly helps across your sales, services, and support teams to deliver an exceptional customer experience.

The three major sections we are going to discuss here are:

A. How to connect Zoho CRM with Zuper?

B. How does Zuper –Zoho CRM Integration work?

C. How to uninstall Zoho CRM from Zuper?

## A. How to connect your Zoho CRM with Zuper?

1. Open a new tab in your browser after logging in to your Zuper Account. Click on your Profile Picture in the top right corner of the screen and select the “**App Store**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=5b5d74b7c119dfd939b2c0c99ec4289b" alt="Zcrm1 Pn" width="1903" height="872" data-path="images/Zcrm1.png" />

4. Under the “**Browse by Category**,” select the “**CRM**” option and choose “**Zoho CRM**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=0e9316419da266c4b367c13a3df09036" alt="Zcrm2 Pn" width="1901" height="921" data-path="images/Zcrm2.png" />

3. Click the “**Install Zoho CRM**” button.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=d2d355cbdea62bbfaeacce7991f54f32" alt="Zcrm3 Pn" width="1901" height="915" data-path="images/Zcrm3.png" />

Switch to the Zuper tab on your browser and log in to your Zuper account.

4. Provide the necessary permission details for the Zoho CRM.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=4e3af70ffdda0e4d303c7ab79d08fd32" alt="Zcrm4 Pn" width="1919" height="919" data-path="images/Zcrm4.png" />

5. Update Zuper Settings by configuring the following fields:

a. Reverse Integration User ID (**Mandatory**) – From the Employees module in Zuper, select the “User,” copy the ID, and paste it into the “**Reverse Integration User ID**.”

b. Zoho Region (**Mandatory**) – Europe, India, and Australia; this will be automatically based on the Zoho regions. You can choose from the drop-down list if the region is not listed automatically.

c. To create the Zuper API key, Click [**here**](https://docs.zuper.co/Settings/Developer_Hub/API_Keys#api-keys) to know how to create and use API keys

<Note>
  **Note**: The update should be auto-synced from Zoho to Zuper properly.
</Note>

We create separate users, with whom we create app keys and use them.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=f36d7d312970a4990674bb9ebb9a3573" alt="Zcrm5 Pn" width="1913" height="918" data-path="images/Zcrm5.png" />

Select the “**Update**” button to connect the Zoho app with Zuper.

## B. How does Zuper–Zoho Integration work?

1. Log in to your Zoho account and create a workflow from Zoho.

The Sync happens from Zoho deal to Zuper Job | Zoho Account as Zuper Organization | Zoho Contact as Zuper Customer. 

| Zoho Entity | Zuper Entity |
| :- | :- |
| Deal | Job |
| Account | Organization |
| Contact | Customer |

## C. How to Uninstall Zoho CRM from Zuper?

3. Click on your Profile Picture in the top right corner of the screen and select the “**App Store**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=5b5d74b7c119dfd939b2c0c99ec4289b" alt="Zcrm1 Pn" width="1903" height="872" data-path="images/Zcrm1.png" />

2. Under the “**Browse by Category**,” select the “**CRM**” option and choose “**Zoho CRM**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=0e9316419da266c4b367c13a3df09036" alt="Zcrm2 Pn" width="1901" height="921" data-path="images/Zcrm2.png" />

3. Click on the “**Uninstall App**” button.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm7.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=e075376c5714205dab68d1dd81fb3c70" alt="Zcrm7 Pn" width="1913" height="918" data-path="images/Zcrm7.png" />

4. The Zoho CRM app is uninstalled successfully.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES/images/Zcrm8.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=M4JqQ68bNxvkxTES&q=85&s=51c62c96fe4e82b5fc0d7254b6bb7c6b" alt="Zcrm8 Pn" width="1909" height="927" data-path="images/Zcrm8.png" />

# Twilio

With the Zuper integration with Twilio, users can now quickly send job status information to customers.

1. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner of the page to open the list of options. Next, click on the “**App Store**” link.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/LSMI4YRakC3vTElg/images/tw1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LSMI4YRakC3vTElg&q=85&s=4de61dc8290a2fb2cb1e593a5145c961" alt="Tw1 Pn" width="1903" height="872" data-path="images/tw1.png" />

2. You will be redirected to “**App Store** ”. Click on “**Telephony, Video & SMS** ” under “**Browse by Category"** on your left. Click the “**Twilio**” card.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/LSMI4YRakC3vTElg/images/twilio5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LSMI4YRakC3vTElg&q=85&s=3d532d5256550fc627e454912de3eb58" alt="Twilio5 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/twilio5.png" />

3. Click on the “**Install Twilio**” button.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/LSMI4YRakC3vTElg/images/twilio3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LSMI4YRakC3vTElg&q=85&s=42b8c05307a8e8db699aef5702a1e97c" alt="Twilio3 Pn" width="1920" height="878" data-path="images/twilio3.png" />

4. Under the “**Update Twilio Settings**” box, enter the “**Account SID**”, “**Auth Token**”,“**Sender Number**” from Twilio.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/LSMI4YRakC3vTElg/images/twilio4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LSMI4YRakC3vTElg&q=85&s=79363d0da580df71c3381ced4fd1a1c1" alt="Twilio4 Pn" width="1887" height="870" data-path="images/twilio4.png" />

<Note>
  **Note**: To get **Account SID** , **Auth Token** , **Sender Number** , Go to your **Twilio Dashboard** and copy the

  **Account SID & Auth Token**.
</Note>

Once you have filled in the details, click the “**Update**” button.

Once you have connected your Twilio account with Zuper, all SMS notifications set up in Zuper will be sent to your customers via the connected Twilio account

# RingCentral 

The **Zuper–RingCentral integration** allows you to connect your RingCentral account with Zuper to seamlessly make calls and send outbound **Email/Text Messages** directly through your RingCentral number.

<Note>
  **Note**:

  * To enable the **RingCentral integration** on your account, contact our Support Team at [**support@zuper.co**](mailto:support@zuper.co).
  * The RingCentral widget remains active and is not refreshed when navigating between Zuper pages.
  * Ensure that Text Message/Text Notifications and Phone Call are enabled for your contacts.

  Navigation: Contacts -> Contacts Listing page -> Edit (via three-dot icon) -> Edit Contact page-> Notification Preferences
</Note>

## A. Connecting RingCentral with Zuper

Follow the steps below to integrate your RingCentral account with Zuper.

1. Log in to your **Zuper web app**. Click your **Profile Picture** in the top-right corner. Select **App Store** from the dropdown menu.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/LSMI4YRakC3vTElg/images/tw1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=LSMI4YRakC3vTElg&q=85&s=4de61dc8290a2fb2cb1e593a5145c961" alt="Tw1 Pn" width="1903" height="872" data-path="images/tw1.png" />

2. Under **Browse by Category**, select **Telephony, Video & SMS**. Choose **RingCentral** from the list of available integrations.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG/images/Ringc2.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG&q=85&s=4cad52abe02ecdb076f3aeecc1dd8276" alt="Ringc2 Jp" width="1892" height="869" data-path="images/Ringc2.jpg" />

3. Click **Install RingCentral**.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG/images/Ringc3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG&q=85&s=e36431b2694c377f44ac8d41a1b1724b" alt="Ringc3 Pn" width="1920" height="827" data-path="images/Ringc3.png" />

3. Enter your **Phone Number** or **Email ID**, and click **Next**. Click **Authorize** to allow Zuper to connect with your RingCentral account.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG/images/Ringc4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG&q=85&s=6bd8aeaab07e750419424e9e643f6ef9" alt="Ringc4 Pn" width="1920" height="827" data-path="images/Ringc4.png" />

1. In the **From Number (Mandatory)** field, enter your **registered RingCentral number**.
   * This is the number from which outbound text messages will be sent.
   * Click **Update** to complete the integration setup.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/abLtqsqQ_ieJMxaO/images/ringc5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=abLtqsqQ_ieJMxaO&q=85&s=1a7f3402892d24ef379cbb756e100e7c" alt="Ringc5 Pn" width="1920" height="827" data-path="images/ringc5.png" />

## B. Using the Zuper–RingCentral Integration

Once the integration is configured, you can send **Email/Text Messages** and make calls directly from Zuper using your RingCentral number.

### Step 1: Access the Email/Text Message Option

1. Navigate to the **Contacts** module from the left navigation menu. Select a contact on the listing page to open its details.

   On the **Contact Details** page, click **More Actions → Email/Text Message**.

Fill in the following fields:

| Field | Description |
| :- | :- |
| **Contact Via** *(Mandatory)* | Select **Text Message** to send a notification. |
| **Contact Number** *(Mandatory)* | Choose the contact’s phone number. |
| **Message Body** *(Mandatory)* | Enter the message content. |

Click **Send** to deliver the text message via RingCentral.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/abLtqsqQ_ieJMxaO/images/ringc8.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=abLtqsqQ_ieJMxaO&q=85&s=5da3409accad3b03fab857ae83c049f9" alt="Ringc8 Pn" width="1898" height="834" data-path="images/ringc8.png" />

### Make Calls via RingCentral

You can initiate calls directly from Zuper, either from the **Job Details** page or the **Contact Details** page.

* On the Job Details or Contact Details page, click **"Call" in** the left panel.
* Choose the contact number you wish to call.

Calls are made using the RingCentral Dialer, which is integrated within Zuper.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/powNjb_XKi8rvfxy/images/ringc10.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=powNjb_XKi8rvfxy&q=85&s=c71bbcb47a248f9270c4d769dea3a0ae" alt="Ringc10 Pn" width="1920" height="827" data-path="images/ringc10.png" />

## C. Uninstalling RingCentral from Zuper

If you no longer need the integration, follow the steps below to uninstall the RingCentral app.

### Access the App Store

1. Log in to your **Zuper web app**. Click your **Profile Picture** in the top-right corner. Select **App Store**.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG/images/Ringc11.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG&q=85&s=587cd3d2c90bbc84cad3b17382ea22e5" alt="Ringc11 Jp" width="1918" height="869" data-path="images/Ringc11.jpg" />

2. Under **Browse by Category**, select **Telephony, Video & SMS**. Choose **RingCentral**. You have the option to Uninstall RingCentral.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG/images/Ringc12.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=r92h1Fvv34DZ61eG&q=85&s=17329c6638911ebcdb8601ef69eabc9d" alt="Ringc12 Jp" width="1892" height="869" data-path="images/Ringc12.jpg" />

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/abLtqsqQ_ieJMxaO/images/ringc12.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=abLtqsqQ_ieJMxaO&q=85&s=a751e185ff73e73c5532c82e91decba1" alt="Ringc12 Pn" width="1920" height="827" data-path="images/ringc12.png" />

A confirmation message will appear once RingCentral has been successfully deactivated and uninstalled.

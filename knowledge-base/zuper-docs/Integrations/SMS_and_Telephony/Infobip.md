# Infobip

With the help of our Zuper-Infobip Integration, business owners can send SMS notifications to their customers with regular updates and reminders for their requested work orders. 

## A. How to connect your Infobip with Zuper? 

1. Open a new tab in your browser and log in to your Zuper Account. Click on your Profile Picture on the top right corner of the screen & select the “**App Store**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/p5yY7PUxHUhdXE-D/images/Stext1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=p5yY7PUxHUhdXE-D&q=85&s=c78ab493af84ab405440a2711c346c96" alt="Stext1 Pn" width="1904" height="872" data-path="images/Stext1.png" />

2. Under the “\*\*Browse by Category,” \*\*select the “**Telephony, Video & SMS**” option and choose “**Infobip**.”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n/images/infobip2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n&q=85&s=a186dd3b8029097a1935ecfbfef9eaad" alt="Infobip2 Pn" width="1918" height="883" data-path="images/infobip2.png" />

 3. Click on the “**Install Infobip**” button.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n/images/infobip3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n&q=85&s=8ea95f6f85b71b09944e05a6ba800f1b" alt="Infobip3 Pn" width="1920" height="884" data-path="images/infobip3.png" />

<Note>
  **Note**: Please keep *Zuper and Infobip* tabs open; you can switch between the tabs for integration. 
</Note>

4. Switch to the Infobip tab on your browser and log in to your Infobip account.

Using the copy icon, copy the API key and API Base URL from the Infobip home page.  

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n/images/infobip4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n&q=85&s=223b5c9d1b071c9369ea6c3e75e4b64b" alt="Infobip4 Pn" width="1920" height="888" data-path="images/infobip4.png" />

## B. Using the Zuper–Infobip Integration

Once the integration is configured, you can send **Text Messages** directly from Zuper using your SimpleTexting number.

1. Navigate to the **Contacts** module from the left panel. Select a contact on the listing page to open its details.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/sy0JlWjPI2ux23uI/images/Cloudtalk9.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=sy0JlWjPI2ux23uI&q=85&s=1f6b2a5ba7bd11ff6f6b5e9dc43eab0b" alt="Cloudtalk9 Pn" width="1916" height="842" data-path="images/Cloudtalk9.png" />

2. On the **Contact Details** page, click **More Actions** (top-right corner) and select **Email/Text Message** from the dropdown menu.

Fill in the following fields:

| **Field** | **Description** |
| :-: | :-: |
| **Contact Via (Mandatory)** | Select **SMS** to send a notification. |
| **Contact Number (Mandatory)** | Choose the contact’s phone number. |
| **Message Body (Mandatory)** | Enter the message content. |

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/YpQ83C8aE_U-mgzA/images/Cloudtalk10.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=YpQ83C8aE_U-mgzA&q=85&s=c1a492f051436055b7758771b2e0a8af" alt="Cloudtalk10 Pn" width="1914" height="874" data-path="images/Cloudtalk10.png" />

Click **Send** to deliver the text message via SimpleTexting.

<Note>
  Note: You can trigger messages via the Contacts module, Bulk Text Message action, or Workflow automation.
</Note>

## C. Uninstalling Infobip from Zuper

If you no longer need the integration, follow the steps below to uninstall the Infobip app.

1. Log in to your **Zuper web app**. Click your **Profile Picture** in the top-right corner. Select **App Store**

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/YpQ83C8aE_U-mgzA/images/Cloudtalk1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=YpQ83C8aE_U-mgzA&q=85&s=2bc9016867f229998cb6d6266d1787fd" alt="Cloudtalk1 Pn" width="1904" height="872" data-path="images/Cloudtalk1.png" />

2. Under **Browse by Category**, select **Telephony, Video & SMS**. Choose **Infobip.** you have the option to Uninstall **Infobip**.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n/images/infobip2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=5SJ69Or7h_Ohe36n&q=85&s=a186dd3b8029097a1935ecfbfef9eaad" alt="Infobip2 Pn" width="1918" height="883" data-path="images/infobip2.png" />

3. The Infobip is uninstalled successfully.

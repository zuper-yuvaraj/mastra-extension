# Direct Messages 

A Direct Message (DM) is a private, one-to-one conversation between users that happens outside of channels. For instance, you can use DMs to quickly ask questions, clarify details with a specific user, or follow up on tasks, jobs, or requests without involving a larger audience.  

<Note>
  **Note**: Admins can initiate direct messages with any user. Field Executives (FEs) and Team Leads (TLs) can start direct messages only with users within their own team. However, they can still access and continue direct messages that have already been initiated by admins. 
</Note>

# Starting a New DM  

To initiate a private conversation with a user in Zuper, follow these steps:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web">
    * Click the **+ icon** in the header. 
    * Select **New Direct Chat** 
    * Search for the member’s name and select them to start a DM.

# Muting a DM 

Muting a conversation helps you minimize distractions by pausing notifications from a direct message. You can still view messages at any time, but they just won’t trigger alerts or show the unread count. 

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web">
    * Hover over the DM in the sidebar 
    * Click the **More actions** (⋮) icon. 
    * Select **Mute this conversation** to stop receiving notifications. 
    * To unmute, repeat the same steps and select **Unmute this conversation**. 

    Alternatively, you can also mute a conversation from its details page. 

    * Open the DM that you want to mute. 
    * Click the More actions (⋮) icon in the DM header and select **Mute Conversation**.

    <iframe className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bCsCiocgZKk5Eq3uu2Wpo6ycxnaO3ii7/preview" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Mobile">
    * In the Chat Home, tap and hold the user name. A pop-up menu appears.
    * Select **Mute conversation** to disable notifications.
    * To unmute, tap and hold again and select **Unmute conversation**.

    Alternatively, you can also mute a conversation from its details page.

    * Open the user name that you want to mute.
    * Tap the username at the top of the chat window.
    * In the conversation details page, **toggle on Mute** **conversation** to disable notifications.

# Pinning a DM 

Pinning a conversation keeps it at the top of your sidebar (Web) or Chat Home (Mobile) under Pinned conversations, making frequently used DMs easy to access. 

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web">
    * Hover over the DM in the sidebar. 

    * Click the More actions (⋮) icon. 

    * Select **Pin this conversation** to keep it at the top of your list. 

    * To unpin, repeat the same steps and select **Unpin this conversation**. 

    Alternatively, you can also pin a conversation from its details page. 

    * Open the DM that you want to pin. 
    * Click the More actions (⋮) icon in the DM header and select **Pin Conversation.**

    * To unpin, tap and hold again and select **Unpin conversation**.

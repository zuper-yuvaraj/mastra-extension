# Insights

> Ask Zuper Sense questions about your business, in your own words, and Sense shows the answer as a KPI, chart, or table, whichever fits best

## What can I ask Sense

* *How did my team perform this quarter?*
* *What does my profitability look like this quarter?*
* *How is my revenue trending?*
* *Which jobs generated the most revenue?*
* *Where are we seeing the highest costs?*

<Tip>
  **Tip**: Use a **suggested prompt** from the prompt library to get started, or ask your own question using text or voice.
</Tip>

The [**Supported Modules**](/Zuper_Sense/Reference/Supported_Modules) page lists every part of Zuper you can ask about, along with example questions for each module.

## How Sense answers

Sense reviews the relevant data in your Zuper account and automatically chooses the best format to present the answer. Depending on your question, the result may appear as a KPI, table, chart, scorecard, or written summary.

## Turn sketches into insights

You can also attach a hand-drawn sketch of the chart or KPI you're picturing. Sense interprets the sketch and recreates it using data from your Zuper account.

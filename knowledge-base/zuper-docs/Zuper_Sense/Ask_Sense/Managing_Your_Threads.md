# Managing your threads

> Start, rename, archive and search your Zuper Sense threads.

Every conversation with Zuper Sense is stored as a thread. Threads maintain their context, allowing you to return days later and seamlessly pick up where you left off.

## Start a thread

1. Open **Sense** from the left navigation.
2. Select **New Thread** in the left panel.
3. Type your question, or pick a suggested prompt below the message box.

<Note>
  **Note:** Sense automatically titles the thread based on your first prompt. Your latest conversations appear under **Recent Threads** in the left panel.
</Note>

## Rename or archive a thread

1. Hover over a conversation under **Recent Threads**.
2. Select the <Icon icon="ellipsis-vertical" />context menu next to the thread name.
3. Select **Rename** to customize the title, or **Archive** to remove it from your active list.

<Warning>
  Archiving removes the thread from your active list. Archive a thread only if you no longer need quick access to it.
</Warning>

## Browse all threads

**Recent Threads** displays your most recent conversations. To view your full history:

1. Select **View all** at the top of the thread list.
2. The **All Threads** page will display all past threads sorted by newest first, along with the timestamp of the last message.

## Find an older thread

You can quickly locate past conversations using either of the following methods:

* **From the All Threads page:** Open **All Threads**, then type a keyword into the main search box to filter your list.
* **From anywhere in Sense:** Use the <Icon icon="search" /> **Search threads** at the top of the left panel to filter past conversations without navigating away from your active thread.

# Changelog

> Sense keeps getting sharper.

Discover what’s new in Zuper Sense, sorted from newest to oldest. Click any update to view its full help page.

<Update label="October 01, 2026" tags={["Radar mobile"]}>
  Radars are now available in the Zuper mobile app, making it easier to monitor key business metrics while on the go.

  * [Radar Mobile](/Zuper_Sense/Radar/Radar_Mobile): Access your personal, prebuilt, and shared Radars directly from **Quick Links** in the Zuper mobile app to view key metrics and insights on the go.
</Update>

<Update label="September 23, 2026" tags={["Skills","Hail Alerts"]} description="Improvements">
  Skills are here: use prebuilt or custom Skills via **slash /** commands, plus hail alerts on Weather Agent, multi-KPI answers, and an All Chats page.

  * [**Skills**](/Zuper_Sense/Agent_Studio/Skills): Browse our library of predefined skills, create your own custom skills, and invoke them directly from Sense Chat using `/` commands. Easily access frequently used instructions right when you need them.
  * [Hail Alerts](/Zuper_Sense/Marketplace_Agents/Weather_Agent): Get hail alerts as part of your weather forecast with the [Weather Agent](/Zuper_Sense/Marketplace_Agents/Weather_Agent), helping you stay informed about potential hail conditions alongside the regular forecast.
  * Sense can now return multiple KPIs in a single response, with the option to add each individual KPI to Radar for ongoing monitoring
  * [All Threads](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/Managing_Your_Threads): A dedicated page to view and reopen all your previous Sense conversations. You can also quickly rename chats directly from the thread list on the left.
</Update>

<Update label="September 11, 2026" tags={["Radar","Drill down","Product Help"]}>
  Radar moves from a single personal view to multiple boards you can share, every card becomes drillable, and Sense starts answering questions about Zuper itself.

  * [**Multiple Radars**](/Zuper_Sense/Radar/Overview), with your previous board carried over as My Radar and two default boards from Zuper, Sales Radar and Finance Radar.
  * [**Radar sharing**](/Zuper_Sense/Radar/Sharing) with Only me, Everyone, or selected people and teams.
  * [**Drill into any card**](/Zuper_Sense/Radar/Setup_Radars#drill-into-a-card), including single-number KPIs, to see the detail behind it.
  * [**Product help**](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/Zuper_Help): ask how to do something in Zuper and get numbered steps with the source articles.
</Update>

<Update label="August 26, 2026" tags={["More Modules"]}>
  Twelve more modules became answerable in Sense, with no setup. Ask about them the same way you already ask about jobs, customers and revenue.

  * Sales and pricing: [**Proposal Options and Pricelists**](/Zuper_Sense/Reference/Supported_Modules#modules-sense-can-answer-on).
  * Inventory and purchasing: [**Parts & Services, Material Requests, Purchase Orders and Vendors**](/Zuper_Sense/Reference/Supported_Modules#modules-sense-can-answer-on).
  * Financials: [**Commissions and Financing**](/Zuper_Sense/Reference/Supported_Modules#modules-sense-can-answer-on).
  * Operations and people: [**Measurements and Timesheets**](/Zuper_Sense/Reference/Supported_Modules#modules-sense-can-answer-on).
  * Customers and properties: [**Organizations and Properties**](/Zuper_Sense/Reference/Supported_Modules#modules-sense-can-answer-on).
  * All Supported Modules: [Supported Modules](/Zuper_Sense/Reference/Supported_Modules)
</Update>

<Update label="August 17, 2026" tags={["Reasoning","Knowledge Base","Agents"]}>
  Sense moves from answering questions to reasoning through them, and two Marketplace agents start doing the checking for you.

  * [**Reasoning**](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/Reasoning): ask why a job was delayed or an invoice is unpaid, and Sense traces the connected records to explain it.
  * [**Knowledge Base**](/Zuper_Sense/Agent_Studio/Knowledge_Base): teach your agents your terminology, policies and calculation rules.
  * [**Weather Agent**](/Zuper_Sense/Marketplace_Agents/Weather_Agent): a daily weather digest for every city your crews work in, with safety and work-impact assessments.
  * [**Reputation Agent**](/Zuper_Sense/Marketplace_Agents/Reputation_Agent): every new Google review, matched to the customer and job it belongs to.
</Update>

<Update label="July 06, 2026" description="Custom Field Analytics">
  Sense now works with your custom fields, not just native ones.

  * **Custom field analytics**: trend, aggregate, filter, and list on custom fields, including cycle time between dates and custom stat cards.
  * **Two-dimensional stacked bar chart**: stack one breakdown inside another with no timeline, like job status stacked within category.
  * **Fixes**: Failed renders and empty results now retry automatically
</Update>

<Update label="May 20, 2026" tags={["Personalization"]}>
  Sense starts remembering how you like things done

  * [**Personalization**:](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/Personalization) set your own preferences for how Sense answers, so future answers are already formatted the way you like.
</Update>

<Update label="May 13, 2026" tags={["Clarifications","Follow Up"]}>
  Sense now asks a clarifying question when needed, suggests follow-ups on every answer, and gets a round of stability fixes

  * [**Clarification questions**:](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/How_Answers_Work) if a question doesn't have enough context, Sense asks before answering instead of guessing.
  * [**Follow-up questions**:](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/How_Answers_Work) every answer can come with suggested follow-up questions, so you can go deeper without starting over.
  * **Fixes**: various stability improvements.
</Update>

<Update label="May 06, 2026" description="Improvements and fixes">
  Open a Radar card straight into chat, and see stacked bar charts as an answer format.

  * **Open in Chat**: open any card on a Radar in chat and keep asking questions from there.
  * **Stacked bar chart support**: Sense can now show an answer as a stacked bar chart.
</Update>

<Update label="April 29, 2026" tags={["Radar"]}>
  Radar arrives as a place to pin answers, and Sense can now read a hand-drawn sketch.

  * [**Radar**](/Zuper_Sense/Radar/Overview): pin any answer from Sense so it's there every time you open it.
  * [**Sketch to Sense**](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/Insights): attach a hand-drawn sketch of the chart or KPI you're picturing, and Sense figures out what you're asking for and shows it in that format.
  * **Pie chart support**: Sense can now show an answer as a pie chart.
</Update>

<Update label="April 16, 2026" description="Launch Week Highlights">
  Zuper Sense launches: the intelligent AI built into Zuper, pulling answers from your business data and showing them as the visualization that fits best

  * [**Insights**](/Zuper_Sense/Ask_Sense/Insights): ask Sense a question and get the answer as a bar chart, line chart, or scorecard, whichever shows it clearest.
</Update>

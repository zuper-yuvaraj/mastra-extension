# Pricing

> Free for now!

Zuper Sense is currently free to use. All actions, including asking questions, running agents, and creating Radars, consume usage credits that are provided at no extra cost while Sense is in free preview.

## How credits work

Credits track your activity behind the scenes. Each Sense action, such as generating a chat response or executing an agent task, consumes a small number of credits. No management or top-ups are required today; Sense handles credit allocation automatically.

<Note>Credit limits and paid pricing tiers are not yet active. This page will be updated with complete plan details and pricing models prior to commercial release.</Note>

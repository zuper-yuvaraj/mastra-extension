# Milestones & dependencies

From the jobs section within the project module, you can manage the entire project
schedule using either the Gantt or List view. Specifically, you can:

* Plan and manage project schedule by creating or adding jobs

* Create milestones to mark significant events as the project progresses

* Create and manage dependencies between jobs and milestones

* Group jobs and milestones into phases

**Creating Jobs, Milestones, and Phases**

You can create jobs, milestones and phases in a project from either the Gantt or List view. Additionally, you can add existing jobs to a project.

## **Create and add jobs to a project**

You can create a new job or add an existing job to the project. To create a new job, follow these steps:

1. Click the "**Create Job**" button at the top right corner of the page.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=572b4f0dd3043ac1883a040c8e1d4966" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil2.png" />2. A sidebar appears on the right with prefilled details such as customer/organization name, service and billing address. You will need to fill in the remaining details, such as the job title, category, due date, user assigned to the job, and other fields.

Click the " **Create** " button to create a new job after filling in all the necessary
details.

## Create a milestone

Milestones can be used to mark significant events or achievements throughout the
project.&#x20;

1. Navigate to the "**Create Job**" button at the top right corner of the page and select "**Create Milestone**" from the dropdown menu. A dialog box will appear.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil10.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=2d74c94e6f1a107d438094c620657635" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil10.png" />2. Enter the following details and click the “**Create**” button to create a milestone.

1. **Phase**: Select the phase you want to add the milestone to from the dropdown.

2. **Name** (Mandatory): Enter the name of the milestone.

3. **Due Date** (Mandatory): Specify the due date for the milestone.

4. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the milestone.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil11.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=76757bec8217980f2d8865db64d2feb4" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil11.png" />

## Create a phase

A phase is a group of jobs and milestones and can be thought of as a sub-project. It is represented as a thick line in the timeline.&#x20;

<Note>
  **Note**: Jobs and milestones not added to any phase can be accessed from the ‘**Not added to any phase**’ section in the Gantt and List view.
</Note>

1. Navigate to the "**Create Job**" button at the top right corner of the page and select
   "**Create Phase**" from the dropdown menu. A dialog box will appear.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil12.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=5914645e2604fc73dd3cd63f51df0a83" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil12.png" />2. Enter the following details and click the “ **Create** ” button to create a phase:

1. **Phase Name**: Enter the name of the phase.

2. **Phase Color**: Choose the color to the phase to visually differentiate it from other phases in the timeline.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil13.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=0583f5ab96d43c2d3b9c15182ddf38bd" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil13.png" />3. Once the phase has been successfully created, you can create job, add existing jobs or drag and drop existing jobs and milestones from the "**Not added to any phase**" section or from other phases, as shown below.

<Note>
  **Note**: The duration of a phase is calculated based on the start date or due date of the earliest job or milestone and the end date or due date of the latest job or milestone in that phase.
</Note>

After creating jobs, milestones and phases in a project, you can access them from either the "**Gantt view**" or the "**List view**". Each has its own benefits:

1. The Gantt view is great for planning your project since you get a clear view of the
   timeline and dependencies.

2. List view on the other hand is great if you want to quickly take a look at all the jobs
   in the project with information such as status, priority etc easily accessible.

### Gantt view

Gantt view provides a bird's eye view of how the project progresses over time. It features a horizontal timeline where each job is represented by a bar, milestones are depicted as diamonds, phases appear as thick lines, and dependencies are shown as arrows connecting two jobs or milestones.

## Accessing jobs, milestones, and phases

To view jobs, milestones and phases, simply click on the desired phase from the left-hand panel. The selected phase, along with its jobs and milestones, will be displayed in the Gantt view.

* A job is represented as a bar. A job bar's **length** corresponds to the **job's** **duration** , and the bar's **position** along the timeline indicates when the **job** **starts and ends**. The **color** of the bar indicates the **job status**. Additionally, you can reschedule the job, create a new note, or detach the job from its phase directly from the Gantt view by right-clicking on it.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil14.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=72cd0380f8a08ac027b06c446efff060" alt="" width="1919" height="1753" data-path="Projects/Mil14.png" />

* Milestones are represented as diamonds in the Gantt view. You can create a milestone directly from the Gantt view, add it to a phase, and create dependencies for it. Additionally, you can edit the milestone, update its status (either "**open**" or "**completed** "), or delete it by right-clicking on it.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil15.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=07ccc921c426280b0fbdd385dc73b64f" alt="" width="1920" height="1751" data-path="Projects/Mil15.png" />

* A phase is a group of jobs and milestones and can be thought of as a sub-project. It is represented as a thick line in the timeline. You can create a phase, add jobs and milestones to it, and edit or delete a phase by right-clicking on it.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil17.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=4afbd631dac907fa90de4b2fb18a9e17" alt="" width="1915" height="877" data-path="Projects/Mil17.png" />

**Managing dependencies**

Dependencies are relationships between jobs based on the order of execution. E.g., Consider if there are two jobs A and B, Job B cannot be started unless Job A is completed, they are dependent on each other. Here, Job B is said to be the ‘**Successor**’ and is ‘**Waiting on**’ Job A, while Job A is said to be the ‘**Predecessor** ’and is said to be ‘ **Blocking** ’ Job B.

Dependencies are indicated by an **arrow** connecting jobs or milestones. The arrow originates from the predecessor and points to the successor.

* To create a dependency between jobs or milestones in the Gantt view, simply drag from one edge of the job bar/milestone to another as shown below. This action visually links the jobs, establishing the sequence and indicating that succeeding jobs cannot start until the preceding jobs are completed.

* To remove a dependency, simply click on the arrow, then confirm by clicking ‘**Remove**’ in the pop-up.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil16.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=eff6f5712705865444a915d0c0aaa48e" alt="" width="1911" height="880" data-path="Projects/Mil16.png" />

* After creating a dependency, you can also reschedule the job/milestone by simply dragging the job bars or milestone diamonds to a new start or end point, as shown below.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil19.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=175d8bd779cb73c4937a5ef459a70195" alt="" width="1914" height="881" data-path="Projects/Mil19.png" />

<Note>
  **Note** : Additionally, when rescheduling, you can manage whether the succeeding jobs' schedule should be changed or remain unchanged.&#x20;
</Note>

1. Click the “ **Configure** ” button at the top right corner and choose “**Dependency Management** ”

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil18.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=f1728618870a5feeb0229e43c9b7be58" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil18.png" />

1. A Dependency Management dialog box appears, where you can choose either of
   the following. Once done, click the “**Save**” button.

   * **Maintain Interval**: This option ensures that the time interval between the dependent jobs remains the same when rescheduling the preceding job.

   * **None**: This option reschedules only the intended job without rescheduling the succeeding jobs.

   * You can also enable/disable the “**Allow scheduling during weekends**” when the dependency management is set to "**Maintain Interval**." When disabled, scheduling on weekends will not be permitted.

Based on the settings, when rescheduling in the Gantt view, the dependent jobs will be
adjusted accordingly.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY/images/mil09.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY&q=85&s=9b4d553a7d2e2f45a2080f69f0699af6" alt="" width="1378" height="635" data-path="images/mil09.jpg" />

<Note>
  **Note** : When rescheduling the preceding job, if the dependent job is scheduled to start before the preceding job ends, the dependency will be highlighted in **red** indicating a **violation** as shown below. Technicians can clock-in to a succeeding job only after the preceding job is completed even if the succeeding job starts before the preceding job ends.
</Note>

**More actions**

You can perform various other actions from the Gantt view.

**Changing the timeline view in the Gantt view**

For improved visibility, you can adjust the view between **month, week, and day** by clicking the dropdown on the top-right of the Gantt.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil20.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=fad67605ca5edbe3fcb7e70f538dd8ff" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil20.png" />

**View job details**

To view any of the job details, click the job in the timeline. A sidebar will appear on the right, displaying the job's primary details and any associated notes.

You can also perform various actions, such as viewing more details about the job, cloning a job, or deleting a job by clicking the "**ellipsis** " icon at the top of the sidebar.

**Access and schedule unscheduled jobs**

You can also quickly access and schedule unscheduled jobs from this view. To do so,

1. Click the " **Unscheduled Jobs** " button at the top right corner. You can view the list of unscheduled job(s) associated with the project.

2. Simply drag and drop the unscheduled job onto the timeline to schedule the job. Once scheduled, the job would be reflected in the "**Not added to any phase**" section.

### List view

The list view displays information in a tabular format, with jobs and milestones grouped under phases.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/NbbApPhcsIqdplnc/Projects/Mil23.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=NbbApPhcsIqdplnc&q=85&s=4c14d2bbcb480076d1e4b72a6af350c8" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil23.png" />

**Quick actions**

Using the quick actions available under the “**Actions**” column, you can perform various actions for phases, milestones, and jobs. Below are the quick actions available for each within the project:

* **Phases**: Create Job, Add Existing Job, Add Milestone, Edit and Delete Phase.

* **Milestones**: Edit, Update status, View dependencies, Delete milestone.

* **Jobs**: View Details, Reschedule Job, View Dependencies and Detach a job.

Detaching a job does not mean permanent deletion. You can still access the job from the “**Jobs**” module. Also, when detaching a job, you'll be prompted to choose whether to:

* Detach the job and keep the associated parts & services, or

* Detach the job and delete its associated parts & services from the project.

1. If you choose to detach the job and keep the parts and services, the job will be removed from the project, but the parts and services consumed will remain in the project under the "**Used Quantity.**" This is helpful if the parts or services were used for other jobs within the project.

2. If you choose to detach the job and delete the parts & services, the job will be
   removed, and the parts and services will be replenished back to the available
   quantity, making them available for other jobs within the project.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/1bNBR6xMzT6dAr4M/images/detach.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=1bNBR6xMzT6dAr4M&q=85&s=e5c06250a5ff5be96fb982f2213e3af7" alt="" width="1380" height="631" data-path="images/detach.jpg" />

## Managing dependencies

In the list view, jobs or milestones with dependencies are marked with a "**Dependency icon**." To manage dependencies, click the dependency icon next to the job or milestone, or select "**Dependencies**" from the quick actions menu.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9/Projects/Mil18.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Gzm-tT_V3nEILwU9&q=85&s=f1728618870a5feeb0229e43c9b7be58" alt="" width="1920" height="878" data-path="Projects/Mil18.png" />

1\. A dialog box will appear, allowing you to view or modify the dependencies.

You can view the predecessors to the job or milestone under "**Waiting on"** (these jobs/milestones need to be completed, canceled or closed for the current job to start). Successors to the job or milestone are listed under "**Blocking**" (these jobs can start only if the current job is completed, canceled or closed).

2\. You can even create dependencies to a job or milestone by clicking the “**+ Add Job** ”
button next to the "**Waiting on**" and "**Blocking**" sections.

<Note>
  **Note**: When creating dependencies in the list view, jobs or milestones that could
  result in a dependency violation not being listed.
</Note>

To remove a dependency, hover over the job or milestone you want to remove and
click on the " **X** " icon near the right edge.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY/images/mil09.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=noPgwpKDoZrQKHPY&q=85&s=9b4d553a7d2e2f45a2080f69f0699af6" alt="" width="1378" height="635" data-path="images/mil09.jpg" />

### Rescheduling a job with dependencies

To reschedule a job with dependencies from the list view, follow these steps:

1. Click " **Reschedule Job** " next to the job with a dependency under the " **Actions** "
   column.

If dependency management is set to “**Maintain interval,**” a pop-up will appear, prompting you to choose between “**Reschedule This Job Only**” or “**Reschedule All Dependent Jobs.**”

When you choose “**Reschedule All Dependent Jobs**,” you will also be able to view the rescheduled dates and times for the dependent jobs as a result of this change.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/NbbApPhcsIqdplnc/Projects/Mil21.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=NbbApPhcsIqdplnc&q=85&s=1aff49abde5dd4e225f24f2938c72642" alt="" width="1920" height="1756" data-path="Projects/Mil21.png" />

## Deleting a phase

When deleting a phase, you will be prompted with the below options:

* **Detach jobs from the project and delete milestones** - This option deletes the phase, detaches jobs from the project (the detached jobs can still be accessed from the Jobs module) and deletes any milestones in the phase.

  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/zuperinc/Nz5hYkeVtSkMQBoe/images/Mil28.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=Nz5hYkeVtSkMQBoe&q=85&s=3be17a6bf0a10efc7e108457473366e0" alt="" width="1920" height="1756" data-path="images/Mil28.png" />

* **Retain jobs and milestones in the project** - This option deletes the phase and retains jobs and milestones in the project. The retained jobs and milestones are moved to the "**Not added to any phase**" section.

Technicians and other users can access jobs and milestones grouped into Phases in a project's “**Jobs**” section. When a job has predecessors that are not yet completed, users cannot clock in to the job, and it will be indicated that the job has dependencies that need to be completed.

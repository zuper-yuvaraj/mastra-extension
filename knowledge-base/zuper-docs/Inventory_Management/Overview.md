# Overview

In Zuper, **Parts, Products, and Services** are essential components used in jobs, quotes, and invoices. These items can be individual goods, services, or bundled offerings that businesses manage within their inventory.

* **Products & Parts** – Tangible items that are either purchased from vendors or consumed during service jobs.

* **Services** – Labor or activities performed for customers that are billable or non-billable.

* **Bundles** – A combination of multiple parts, products, or services grouped together for streamlined pricing and inventory tracking.

Zuper helps businesses efficiently track inventory by automatically adjusting stock levels when items are used in jobs or transfers between locations.
